## Plain English

Each split has a few lines: safe and slow, or fast and risky. We try **every combination** that is legal on 1.0.1.2 (24 routes) on the same 100,000 simulated runs, and keep the one with the best chance of beating 6:50. Then we ask: *which single thing, practised, raises that chance most?*

## Objective

```
route* = argmax over routes of P(T_route < 6:50.00)
```

If even the default route gives P(<6:50) < 1% for a profile, the ranking would be pure noise. The objective then switches to **P(beat your stretch goal)**, where the stretch goal is that profile's own 10th-percentile time: your next milestone. ΔP(<6:50) is still reported for every lever.

Splits are independent, but "sum < 6:50" is not separable. A line that is faster on average but riskier can still lower P(beat) if you are already near the goal, so we enumerate the routes instead of choosing per split.

## Practice priorities

The levers, each evaluated with common random numbers on the best **proven-only** route:

- **skip**: halve that skip's miss rate. Shows the clean reps needed to move the Beta mean there.
- **pinned-save**: one of Nanobit's own pinned "lost time here" notes from his 7:01, applied as seconds off the split.
- **pace / consistency**: 1 s off a split's clean median, or 25% less spread.
- **learn-line**: switch one split to a line you don't run, with its skips at ≥ 90%. Lines marked unverified must be tested on 1.0.1.2 first.
