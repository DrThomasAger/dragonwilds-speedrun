## Snare (Ranged 12)

Ground trap that roots enemies that walk into it for 8 s; 45 s cooldown.

- Unlocked at level 12 Ranged; costs 15 astral + 10 nature runes. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Snare)
- Places a floating star with a circular trap; enemies that run/walk to the star are stunned for eight seconds. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Snare)
- Can be triggered remotely by shooting the star (ranged/magic) while monsters are in the circle; melee attacks cannot trigger it. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Snare) [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Snare)
- Venomous Trapper (Ranged 40) adds a poison cloud to the trap. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Snare)
- Cooldown reduced to 45 s in 1.0 (was 60 s). [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Snare)
- Usable inside vaults. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Snare)
- Boss interaction: no source documents whether bosses can be snared - unknown. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Snare)
- Recommended for crowd control, e.g. lesser demons in Dowdun Reach. [SuperStooob 10:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y9d6cBv4hfU&t=653s)

## Confuse (Magic 30)

Makes an aggressive enemy fight other monsters for 45 s; not on bosses/minibosses.

- Magic 30, 25 astral runes, 120 s cooldown. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Confuse)
- Target attacks other nearby monsters for 45 seconds or until it dies; attacking it yourself breaks the spell and it retargets you. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Confuse) [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Confuse)
- Cast range 40 m; only affects aggressive monsters within a 30 m radius; you get loot from monsters the confused enemy kills. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Confuse) [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Confuse)
- Does not work on Garou thane, Dire wolf, KotHaar (need Greater Confuse), training dummy, or boss and miniboss monsters. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Confuse) [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Confuse)
- Explicitly immune: Goblin Thug, Rotsworn Reanimator, Aggressive Terrorbird. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Aggressive_Terrorbird) [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Goblin_Thug)
- Quest note: for Biohazard, corrupted ravens must be killed directly, not via Confuse. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Corrupted_Raven)
- Turns one of a group against the rest - cuts incoming damage as well as adding damage. [SuperStooob 11:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y9d6cBv4hfU&t=675s)

## Greater Confuse (Magic 44 perk)

Extends Confuse to stronger creatures (thanes, Garou King, dire wolves, KotHaar); still not zogres, dragons, Tok-Xil.

- Unlocked at Magic 44; lets Confuse hit Garou thane, Garou King, Dire wolf. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Greater_Confuse)
- Mini-bosses such as zogres, dragons and Tok-Xils are still unaffected. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Greater_Confuse)
- 0.10 and 0.11 added more monsters to the Greater-Confuse-only list. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Greater_Confuse)

## Parry and stagger

Last-moment block staggers most enemies (~3 s) for +25% damage; bosses like Velgar are stagger-immune.

- Parry unlocks at Attack 5: block at the last moment to stagger the attacker for roughly 3 seconds. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Parry)
- Bosses such as Velgar are not staggered (Stagger page lists General Velgar: Immune). [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Parry)
- After a parry there is a minimum 0.550 s before you can attack. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Parry)
- Parrying regains some stamina. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Combat)
- Enemies can be staggered by abyssal whip special, warhammer special, or parry; staggered targets take 25% more damage. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Stagger) [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Stagger)
- Enemies need 0.5 s after a stagger ends to recover; melee set bonus does not apply during that recovery. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Stagger)
- You get staggered by blocking with no stamina or blocking an unblockable without Tempest Shield (e.g. Garou berserker red attack, vault hulk double slam). [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Stagger) [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Stagger)
- Some boss-type enemies stagger at 50% HP, e.g. Abyssal demon. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Stagger)
- Shielded garou (thane/King) block everything until their stance breaks (thane: 3 s stagger), then attack while stagger-immune. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Garou_Thane)
- Video claim: a successful parry refunds 50% of MAX stamina and opens a visceral (cinematic punish) attack; powerful telegraphed attacks cannot be parried - dodge those. [SuperStooob 3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y9d6cBv4hfU&t=208s) [SuperStooob 3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y9d6cBv4hfU&t=228s)
- Second source (GamerCory) agrees: successful parry restores 50% of max stamina and can open a visceral follow-up. [GamerCory 0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pCa0josBReo&t=52s) [GamerCory 1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pCa0josBReo&t=96s)

## Tempest Shield (Attack 11)

Air shield that blocks ALL damage while blocking (including unblockables); burst knockdown at end.

- Prevents all damage while blocking for a limited time; triggers a burst when it ends. 45 s cooldown (1.0). [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Tempest_Shield) [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Tempest_Shield)
- End burst knocks nearby foes to the ground if the shield is raised. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Tempest_Shield)
- Cast before an encounter; there is rarely time once in battle. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Tempest_Shield)
- Blocks "unblockable" boss attacks (e.g. Black Knight Titan Leap/overheads). [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Velgar/Strategies)

## Surge / Afterimage

Three 8 m stamina-free dodges; Afterimage decoy pulls aggro for 10 s.

- Surge (Magic 11) gives three Surge dodges for two minutes; each travels eight metres. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Surge) [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Surge)
- Afterimage (Magic 25): first Surge charge leaves a decoy for 10 s that enemies attack instead. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Afterimage)
- Strategy pages: Surge dodges cost no stamina. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Velgar/Strategies)

## Stamina, dodging, blocking

Stamina gates attacks, dodges and blocks; hydration sets regen, rest sets max.

- Dodge costs 20, jump 10, running 4/s; successful shield block 17 (holding block is free). [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Stamina)
- Regeneration starts after 3 s without stamina actions; magic 1/3 set bonus halves the delay. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Stamina)
- Hydration sets regen: 0.75/s at 0 up to 6.0/s at 100 hydration; low rest caps max stamina (80/60/40). [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Stamina)
- Dodging can animation-cancel most player animations (e.g. cast Abyssal Snare then dodge). [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Stamina)
- Keep enough stamina in reserve for one dodge or block. [Thurnax 3:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IUMO59unHgs&t=205s)
- Common dodge mistake: dodging too early; enemies wind up, attack, recover. Dodge sideways to stay in counter range. [Thurnax 4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IUMO59unHgs&t=266s) [Thurnax 4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IUMO59unHgs&t=292s)
- Bosses: stand at their sides rather than in front, which gives them access to every attack. [Thurnax 7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IUMO59unHgs&t=451s)
- Use a crossbow to pull enemies one at a time. [Thurnax 6:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IUMO59unHgs&t=362s)
- One magic armour piece gives -50% stamina regen delay; 2 melee + 1 magic recommended for melee. [SuperStooob 0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y9d6cBv4hfU&t=54s)
- Since 1.0 weapon specials use a separate special-attack energy, not stamina. [GamerCory 2:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pCa0josBReo&t=141s)
- Several spells (Enchant Weapon, Windstep, Surge) can be cast while moving. [GamerCory 5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pCa0josBReo&t=316s)
- Sprint attacks (Attack 13) give a lunging opener after a short sprint. [GamerCory 4:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pCa0josBReo&t=258s)

Melee = slash/stab/crush; magic = air/fire/water (+earth); weak spots crit with no cooldown.

- Melee weapons deal slash, stab or crush; ranged one type; spells air, fire, water. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Combat) [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Combat)
- Hitsplats show critical, resistance and weakness damage. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Combat)
- Weak spots give critical damage with no crit cooldown. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Combat)
- Air magic: high single-target damage and knockdown; water magic slows and heals; fire magic AoE and builds Burning. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Combat) [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Combat)

- Opening on an unaware enemy (e.g. longbow) gives a damage bonus; wind spells knock down/stagger groups for melee follow-up; whetstone/magical focus = 15% damage for 10 minutes. ## Poison

DoT of 3 or 4 HP/s; natural recovery costs ~half your HP; antipoison/Fungal Fortitude.

- Inflicted poison deals 3 or 4 damage per second depending on source; natural recovery deals roughly half the health bar. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Poison) [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Poison)
- Antipoison, Antipoison II and Fungal Fortitude prevent environmental buildup and resist poison attacks. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Poison)
- Players can poison via weapon poison or by parrying with the dragonblight shield. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Poison)

## Burning

8 HP/s for 6 s (48 total); antifire prevents; dodge-roll or water shortens.

- Burning deals 8 damage per second for 6 seconds (48 total). [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Burning)
- Avoided with antifire potions; duration reduced by dodge rolling or entering water. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Burning)
- Antifire perk removes Burning stacks and grants 33% resistance to buildup; 300 s (antifire) / 600 s (super). [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Antifire)
- Velgar, Imaru, Black Knight Titan, Fuzan, KotHaar and dragons are burn-immune - skip fire runes/arrows there. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Velgar/Strategies)

## Bleed

Meter that bursts for 30-32 damage when full.

- Builds a meter; deals 30-32 damage instantly when full. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Bleed)
- Players apply bleed with weapon barbs / barbed arrows (Ranged 22) or abyssal whip (25 bleed build-up). [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Combat)

## Shocked

Meter that empties your stamina when full.

- From wild lapulis vines and lightning (Stormtouched Highlands, Umbral Sands sandstorms); full meter completely drains stamina. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Shocked)

## Wither / Soul Rift

Fellhollow meter; full = Soul Rift (needed to hurt Imaru's spectral form).

- Builds from withered water/ground or Rotsworn attacks; when full you enter a Soul Rift state; Steadfast Stomach slows it. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Wither) [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Wither)
- Running out of time while Soul Rifted is instant death; while rifted you cannot heal (Imaru strategy). [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Imaru/Strategies)

## Scorch (Umbral Sands)

Sunlight meter; full drains 1 HP + 1.5 hydration per second; 5 resistance sources = immune.

- Builds in direct sunlight in Umbral Sands, falls off in shade or at night; when full drains 1 health and 1.5 hydration per second. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Scorch)
- Time in sun before Scorch: 0 sources 80 s, 1: 105 s, 2: 160 s, 3: 330 s, 4: 480 s, 5: full protection (desert armour, Frosted Flavours, cooling infusions, monkfish skewers, Umbral kebab stack). [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Scorch)
- Buildup about halved near sunrise (~7:30) and sunset (~20:00). [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Scorch)
- Bug: with <5 sources, building scorch then swapping to 5 and stepping into shade builds instead of lowering. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Scorch)

## Boss / spawning rules

Generic boss behaviour and respawn rules.

- Lodestones can interfere with certain bosses spawning (Garou King). [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Boss)
- Monsters respawn in 1-3 in-game days depending on type/location (many 1.0 elites list 4 days; zogres 8 days). [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Spawning) [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Zogre)
- Abyssal demons teleport to the player if too far away or in an area they cannot reach (anti-safespot). [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Abyssal_Demon)
- Ring of life teleports you to your bed at 1 HP or 10% HP (bail-out in boss fights). [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Combat)
- Lifeward/overhealth was removed in 0.12.0. [RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Combat)
