## Dive cancel (jump-attack / plunge cancel)

Also called: plunge-cancel, jump attack cancel (JAC), dive cancel, windstep double jump (community name, probably the same family)

- **Category:** movement · **Where:** anywhere; used throughout the raid route
- **Found by:** Dotxy (Dotxygaming) · **First seen:** 2026-10-14 [Dotxy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LGCddmk_TnM)
- **Needs:** Windstep or Double Jump (for height), Jump Attack (added in 1.0.1), any targeted AoE spell (e.g. Magical Mending), a weapon with a plunge animation (not Dragon Scimitar)
- **Saves:** unknown in seconds overall; Nanobit estimates "saving a second maybe" at one spot of his 7:01
- **Risk:** Dragon Scimitar has no plunge animation per Nanobit, so equip another weapon; needs a targeted AoE spell on the ring
- **How:**
  1. Get high enough for a Jump Attack (after Windstep or a double jump).
  2. Press attack to start the Jump Attack (plunge).
  3. During the animation cast any targeted AoE spell from the spell ring.
  4. Cancel the cast: full movement returns instantly (no landing lag).
- **Status by patch:**
  - 1.0.1: **works** — Published the day 1.0.1 shipped; Nanobit used it in his 1.0.1 8:32 and 1.0.1/1.0.1.1 7:01 runs. [Dotxy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LGCddmk_TnM) [Nano](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw_Mr5TGJAg)
  - 1.0.1.1: **works** — Nanobit 7:01 (1.0.1/1.0.1.1) credits it. [Nano](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doVQ9Ph256g) [@iNanobit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doVQ9Ph256g)
  - 1.0.1.2: **works** — Dotxy's 1.0.1.2 Sanctum flight is built on it; IINo1goodII (post-1.0.1.2) used "the plunge mech" to bypass patched rocks. [Dotxy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GeWbo4GpFEg) [@IINo1goodII](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hncfjhUvg8&lc=UgzsG9pcWMgrsDvaX2R4AaABAg)

- **Saves:** Strong zoops remove the need for Air/Phase Dash on several gaps; ~2.5 s (Keep wall -> gate) per Nanobit; "A LOT of time" on the Keep zoop
