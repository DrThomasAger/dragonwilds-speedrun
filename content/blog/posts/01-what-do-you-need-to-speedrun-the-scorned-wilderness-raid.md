**Raid-ready** is the state where your character owns **every unlock the record route uses** before the timer starts: the **Agility movement kit**, three **spells**, one **combat perk**, a short list of **consumables** and the **quest gate** that opens the raid.

This pillar turns that list into **five short posts**. Each one names the requirement, the **level or cost**, and **how to get it**, with the source quoted.

## Why do requirements come first?

**Every skip in the current route is built from unlocks.** The Fragments zoop uses **Air Dash**, the Abyssal Demon skip uses **Surge** and **Phase Dash**, and ***Dotxy's*** Sanctum flight uses **Windstep**, the **Jump Attack** and a **targeted AoE spell** on your ring.

The wiki states the rule for the whole kit: Agility *"unlocks the whole movement kit: Windstep, Double Jump, Air Dash, Phase Dash and Recall"* ([Agility, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Agility)). **Level the character first**, then learn the route.

## Where does each unlock land on a fresh account?

The ***Raid Lab*** Hardcore 1 to Max route places every unlock in a phase, and the build sheets in the Raid Lab repo follow the same order:

| Phase | Region | Unlocks for the raid |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | Temple Woods | Windstep, Double Jump, Air runes (Rune Mysteries), Leggings of Lightness |
| **2** | Ghornfell | Tempest Shield, Surge, Air Dash, Superheat (Artisan 20) |
| **3** | Fellhollow | Phase Dash, Magical Mending (Artisan 56) |
| **4** | Dowdun Reach | Recall, *Death From Above!* (Attack 25, placed between Attack 11 and 30) |
| **5** | Umbral Sands | Reactive Recall, Enfeeble, Icthlarin's Little Helper complete |
| **6** | Scorned Wilderness | Regicide started, Fleet-footed Infusion, Strong Tea |

**Law runes are the one source conflict.** The wiki states *"Law runes can be crafted at a rune altar at level 50 Runecrafting or higher"* ([Law Rune, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Law_Rune)), while the Hardcore route lists Runecrafting 16. **Check it in-game**, and stock up from **Garou Packs**, which drop **5 to 10 Law runes** at a **10/100** rate ([Garou Pack, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Garou_Pack)).

## Which build should you follow?

The Raid Lab publishes **four builds**, each with its stats, items, ring loadout, keybinds and pre-run state:

| Build | For | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **1.0.1.2 record route (flight)** | PC runners going for sub-6:50 | Theory: ***Nanobit's*** 7:01 route plus ***Dotxy's*** flight; no posted run uses it yet |
| **Console 1.0.1.2 route** | PlayStation and Xbox | Works: ***FastTipsGaming's*** full unedited raid, 2 November |
| **Safe learning route** | New runners, one skip at a time | ***SamFPlays'*** 1.0.1 follow-along; re-test Vines to Keep on 1.0.1.2 |
| **Hardcore-safe route** | One-gravestone characters | Repo plan with Reactive Recall always on |

**Start on the safe learning route**, then move to the record route once the zoops and the flight loop land on demand.

## What this means when you run

- **Reach Agility 44 and Magic 11** before your first timed attempt.
- **Put Magical Mending on your ring** and unlock *Death From Above!* at Attack 25.
- **Pack a Fleet-footed Infusion** every single run.
