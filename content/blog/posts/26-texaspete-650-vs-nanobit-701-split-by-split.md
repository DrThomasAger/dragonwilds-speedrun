The **6:50 versus the 7:01** is the comparison of the top two Scorned Wilderness runs on one shared trigger: ***TexasPete*** gains **23.7 seconds** in the first three splits, and ***Nanobit*** takes back **11.73 seconds** in Cataclysm Keep and Kuldra's Sanctum. The record margin is **11.97 seconds**.

**The two runs sit on different patches.** ***TexasPete*** played on 14 October, *"prior to the windstep double jump and some other new routing strats"*; ***Nanobit*** ran 1.0.1/1.0.1.1 and notes the 6:50 *"is using some strats that have been patched at this point"*.

## Why is TexasPete faster early?

**The Stronghold holds the biggest gap: 11.4 seconds.** ***Nanobit*** names his own losses there: an *"Extra swing, not ideal"* at the Thane and a forgotten infusion, *"Don't forget to re-use your fleet-footed infusion here, because I did."* ([3:22](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=202)). ***Sir_Rebral*** saw the same moment: *"the extra hit after the Thane kill broke my heart haha"*.

**TexasPete commits to the big jumps.** His commentary at the Keep needle: *"Here at the needle."* ([4:27](https://youtu.be/6WEIHo7AUf0?t=267)), then *"I'm going to risk it all"* ([5:28](https://youtu.be/6WEIHo7AUf0?t=328)). Some of those landings are gone on 1.0.1.2; ***Keimbho***: *"the new update made the rock a phantom."*

## Why is Nanobit faster late?

**The hole in Keep.** ***Nanobit*** thanks ***notmusty1*** via ***IINo1goodII*** *"for finding a lovely hole in Keep!"*, and his Keep through that hole is **8.5 seconds** faster than TexasPete's. In the Sanctum he wins **3.23 seconds**. Both Sanctum segments climb the blocks to Kuldra, which 1.0.1.2 closed, so a run today flies that stretch with ***Dotxy's*** loop.

## What this means when you run

- **Match TexasPete's pace** through the Stronghold with today's skips.
- **Take Nanobit's Keep hole** and his Sanctum line for the back half.
- **Bank 8 seconds by the Keep exit**, because flight gives about 6 back in the Sanctum.
