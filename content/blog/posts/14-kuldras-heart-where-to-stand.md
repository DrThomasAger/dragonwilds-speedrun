**Kuldra's Heart** is the **final target** of the raid, inside Kuldra, and the run ends on the frame the screen goes **black after the "Get out now!" timer** that starts when the Heart dies. The record finish is the **melee safespot** *(a standing spot beside the Heart where you take no hits)*, which ***TexasPete*** brought to the route and ***Nanobit*** uses in his 7:01.

The Heart is a **DPS check** *(a fight decided by how much damage you deal per second)*, and **heartworms** *(parasites with 753 HP that keep respawning in the chamber)* guard it. The melee safespot finishes it in about **46 s**. ***SamFPlays'*** magic spot with a 2H mage staff takes about **92 s** and is the calm way to learn the room.

## Where is the melee safespot?

**The melee Heart** is ***TexasPete's*** idea, credited by ***Nanobit***. ***Nanobit*** slightly missed the safespot at 6:47 in his 7:01 and jumped in correctly at **6:53**, about **6 s** lost.

1. **Leave the Heart tunnel** and jump straight into the safespot along the line ***Nanobit*** takes at [6:53](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=413).
2. **Burn the Heart** from there.
3. **Run for the exit** the moment "Get out now!" appears: the timer runs until the screen goes black.

## Where do you stand with magic?

**Stand back at the edge.** Three runners describe the same spot:

1. **Follow ***SamFPlays'*** standing spot** from his chapter [Where to stand so heartworms don't spawn](https://youtu.be/ESrrkdxD1fo?t=637).
2. **Stay up on the edge** and cast at the Heart from there. ***southpark159753***: *"You can stand back and hit the heart with magic, doesnt start the fight with worms. Like dont drop down into the area."*
3. **Hold your spot close to the edge.** ***beandon*** explains the reason: standing too far in lets a tentacle spawn on you.

## How fast is the Heart?

**The Heart is timed in two pieces** (about ±0.3 s): from the **Heart tunnel load** to the **"GET OUT NOW" banner**, then from the banner to the **cut to black**.

| Segment | ***TexasPete*** 6:50 | ***Nanobit*** 7:01 | ***TexasPete*** 7:40 | ***SamFPlays*** 10:53 | Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tunnel to Heart dead | 47.8 (melee) | **46.0** (melee) | 53.4 (melee) | 91.6 (mage staff) | 42.5 |
| Escape to the cut to black | **13.8** | 14.2 | 14.0 | 13.9 | 13.8 |

**The melee safespot saves 45.6 s** against the mage staff from range. **The target is 42.5 s**: ***Nanobit's*** 46.0 s with the safespot entered first time. **The escape is fixed** at about 13.8 to 14.2 s in every run, so the time to gain lives in the Heart itself.

## Does it work on 1.0.1.2?

**Both spots work on 1.0.1.2.** ***beandon*** posted his edge advice on 27 October and ***southpark159753*** his magic spot after the patch went live.

## What this means when you run

- **Jump straight into the melee safespot** on your first try: that is 6 s against the 7:01.
- **Learn the room with the magic spot** at the edge, then switch to melee for timed runs.
- **Run the moment the Heart dies**: the escape is a fixed 14 s and the clock stops on the cut to black.
