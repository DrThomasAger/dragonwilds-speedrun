**The raid gate** is the quest requirement that opens the Scorned Wilderness: **complete Icthlarin's Little Helper and start Regicide**. With that done, the raid entrance in the **north-western Fractured Plains** of Ghornfell takes you to **Rasthin's Rest**, the hub where every run begins.

This post gets you from **quest log** to **first timer start**.

## What do you need?

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Icthlarin's Little Helper** | Main-story quest; complete it. |
| **Regicide** | The next main-story quest; start it. |
| **Rasthin's Rest** | The raid hub and the only place you can build. |
| **Commander Zilyana** | The NPC who starts or resumes a run. |
| **Zilyana Teleporter** | The extraction point at the end of each segment. |

The wiki lists the gate as *"Complete Icthlarin's Little Helper; start Regicide"* and the entrance as *"North-western Fractured Plains, Ghornfell"* ([Scorned Wilderness, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Scorned_Wilderness)). speedrun.com tracks both quests as **Primary Quest** values on its Any% board, so they sit on the **main story line** ([speedrun.com](https://www.speedrun.com/Runescape_Dragonwilds)).

## How do you get in?

1. **Finish Icthlarin's Little Helper** in the main quest line. Its final step is **defeating Fuzan** in the Umbral Sands ([Icthlarin's Little Helper, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Icthlarin's_Little_Helper)).
2. **Start Regicide** at the **Dragon Gate**: the wiki's start is *"Interact with the Dragon Gate north of the Fractured Plains."* ([Regicide, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Regicide)).
3. **Go through to the Scorned Wilderness** from the north-western Fractured Plains in Ghornfell.
4. **Arrive at Rasthin's Rest**, the hub.
5. **Set your bed spawn here** and **sleep** before your first attempt ([setup checklist](/raid-speedrun-setup-checklist/)).
6. **Talk to Commander Zilyana** to **start a new run**. The speedrun timer starts on the first frame the loading icon disappears.

## How does Rasthin's Rest work?

**Rasthin's Rest** is the **safe hub** at the raid's edge. Jagex added it with 1.0 as *"a new area which players can build in, and the Raid area which does not permit building, because of the big dragon"* ([1.0 notes, Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1374490/view/1843481262704158)).

**Commander Zilyana** **starts or resumes runs**, and raid deaths place your **gravestone near her** ([Rasthin's Rest, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Rasthin%27s_Rest)). Since 1.0.0.5, *"Summon Shelter will be able to be used in the Scorned Wilderness hub"* ([1.0.0.5 notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1374490/view/1844751498218499)), so you **rest right at the start line**.

## What are the five splits?

Each raid segment ends at a **Zilyana Teleporter**, the extraction point that saves your progress. The community split file uses the five segments:

1. **Dragontail Vines**
2. **Fragments of Ghornfell**
3. **The Shattered Stronghold**
4. **Cataclysm Keep**
5. **Kuldra's Sanctum**

**Splits 1 to 4** end on the **"Extraction point unlocked" banner**. **Split 5** ends on the **cut to black** after *"Get out now!"* ([speedrun.com Raid rules](https://www.speedrun.com/Runescape_Dragonwilds)).

## What this means when you run

- **Start Regicide** once, then the raid stays open for every attempt.
- **Set your bed at Rasthin's Rest** so every respawn lands at the start line.
- **Split on the extraction banner** for segments 1 to 4 and on the cut to black for segment 5.
