The **skip-level optimal path** is the fastest route through the Scorned Wilderness built from **13 checkpoint pieces**, each one the quickest version of that stretch that **still works on patch 1.0.1.2**. Stitched together, the pieces add up to **6:52.80**, and a **6:48.10** pace on top of them beats the record.

**Every existing run has a patched piece in it.** The 6:50 uses the old Keep skip, the Thane-less wall climb and the Windstep up the blocks to Kuldra; the 7:01 and SamFPlays' run also climb the blocks. So the path is built piece by piece, and every piece without a 1.0.1.2 run behind it is marked **unverified**.

## How was the path measured?

**Frame by frame, on 15 checkpoints.** Each checkpoint time was *"read off contact sheets (1 frame / 2 s) and refined with 0.2 s strips (about +-0.3 s, about +-1 s on fuzzy visual checkpoints)"*. The checkpoints follow ***SamFPlays'*** chapters, so every run cuts at the same places.

| Number | Time | What it includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Sum of best, any patch** | 6:35.70 (395.7 s) | Fastest version of every piece, patched or not |
| **1.0.1.2 composite** | **6:52.80 (412.8 s)** | Pieces that work on 1.0.1.2, three of them unverified |
| **Strict composite** | 7:10.10 (430.1 s) | Proven 1.0.1.x pieces only: ***Nanobit's*** Fragments zoop and Thane kill, ***Dotxy's*** flight |
| **Target pace** | **6:48.10 (408.1 s)** | The composite minus 4.7 s of named saves |

## What are the three unverified pieces?

1. **The TexasPete line to Fragments** (9.5 s): straight off the black dragon ledge, a 1.0.0.6-era line that no 1.0.1 runner has shown. **Fallback:** ***Nanobit's*** vines zoop, 15.6 s.
2. **The FastTipsGaming Thane launch** (51.9 s): no Thane kill; climb the rocks left of the air door, Windstep and glide into the forest. It comes from a full raid posted on 2 November, after 1.0.1.2, timed at tutorial pace. **Fallback:** ***Nanobit's*** Thane kill and air door, 63.1 s.
3. **The Dotxy flight** (51.9 s): a demo, which lands and walks the walkway mid-way. **It replaces the blocks route**, 35.0 s in both records, which 1.0.1.2 closed.

**Test each unverified piece** three times on 1.0.1.2 before you route it.

## What pace beats 6:50 on this path?

The target is **6:48.10**: the composite minus **4.7 seconds** of named saves.

| Split | Target | ***TexasPete*** 6:50 | ***Nanobit*** 7:01 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1 Fragments** | **1:22.70** | 1:21.52 | 1:27.82 |
| **S2 Portal** | **2:23.20** | 2:22.62 | 2:34.92 |
| **S3 Forest** | **3:15.70** | 3:14.32 | 3:38.02 |
| **S4 Keep exit** | **4:42.30** | 4:50.62 | 5:05.82 |
| **END** | **6:48.10** | 6:50.00 | 7:01.97 |

**Where the 4.7 seconds come from:**

- **Sanctum flight, 51.9 to 49.0 s.** A direct flight line skips the demo's walk.
- **Heart, 46.0 to 42.5 s.** ***Nanobit*** *"slightly missed the melee safespot"*; hit it first time.
- **Keep, 75.2 to 73.5 s.** Plunge cancels at 5:07 and 5:12, no extra rock jump, an instant rib clip.

**The pace shape:** level with the 6:50 through S3, about **8 s ahead at S4** thanks to the Keep hole, then about **6 s back in the Sanctum**, because flight is slower than the patched blocks. **The Sanctum flight is the swing segment.**

## What this means when you run

- **Load the 6:48.10 split targets** into your timer and race them every attempt.
- **Test the three unverified pieces** on 1.0.1.2, keeping the fallback for each.
- **Drill the Sanctum flight** toward 49.0 s door to tunnel, the swing segment.
