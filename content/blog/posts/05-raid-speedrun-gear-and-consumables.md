**The raid speedrun loadout** is the short list of **items and drinks** the record route carries: a **Fleet-footed Infusion**, **Strong Tea**, your **best mace**, a **two-handed mage staff** and your **best mage armour**. ***SamFPlays*** published it with his skip guide, and every post-1.0.1 route uses it.

## What goes in the backpack?

| Item | Effect | How to get it | Why the route uses it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fleet-footed Infusion** | Sprint costs **50% less stamina** for **300 s**; +40 hydration | **1 Clean Water + 1 Feathers** at a Stone or Cooking Range | Sprint-heavy route, every split |
| **Strong Tea** | **+20 Rest**, +10 hydration | **1 Ground Tea Leaves + 3 Clean Water** at a Cooking Range | Rest restore inside the raid |
| **Best mace** | Melee, crush damage | Smith your best tier | Kills the air Garou Thane through the gate |
| **2H mage staff** | Magic damage | Craft your best tier | Burns Kuldra's Heart |
| **Best mage armour** | Magic damage and defence | Craft your best tier | Heart phase damage check |
| **Leggings of Lightness** (optional) | Windstep costs **0 runes** | **6 Coarse Animal Fur + 6 Rune Essence** at a Crafting Table | Free Windsteps |
| **Antipoison** | Cures poison | Craft or loot | Heart phase poison |

Recipes and effects come from the wiki pages cited below.

## Why is the Fleet-footed Infusion mandatory?

**The Fleet-footed Infusion** grants the **Fleet Footed** perk: *sprinting costs 50% less stamina for 300 seconds* ([Fleet-footed infusion, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Fleet-footed_infusion)). ***SamFPlays*** puts it first: *"Fleet Foot Infusion: MUST BRING. Cuts sprinting stamina cost by 50%."* ([Every Skip Explained, 1:43](https://youtu.be/ESrrkdxD1fo?t=103)).

**300 seconds is shorter than a 6:50 run.** ***Nanobit*** re-drinks it in the Stronghold: *"Don't forget to re-use your fleet-footed infusion here, because I did."* ([7:01, 3:22](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=202)). **Carry two.**

**Infusions share one slot.** The wiki's Kuldra strategy page notes they do not stack with each other, so the Fleet-footed Infusion replaces a Pumpkin Spice Infusion ([Kuldra/Strategies, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Kuldra/Strategies)).

## Why bring Strong Tea?

**Strong Tea** restores **20 Rest**, and inside the raid it is your **only rest source**: the wiki states *"Resting cannot be performed during combat or a raid"* ([Rest, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Rest)). ***SamFPlays*** rates it *"nice to have, restores +20 Rest"* ([1:43](https://youtu.be/ESrrkdxD1fo?t=103)).

## Why a mace, a staff and mage armour?

**The mace** kills the **air Garou Thane through the closed gate** on ***Sir_Rebral's*** Stronghold route. The Garou Thane's weakness is **crush** ([Garou Thane, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Garou_Thane)), which a mace deals. ***SamFPlays***: *"Melee weapon: Best mace you have"*.

**The staff and armour** finish the run. ***SamFPlays***: *"Mage weapon: 2-handed mage staff for killing the heart at the end."* and *"Armour: best mage gear you have."* ([1:43](https://youtu.be/ESrrkdxD1fo?t=103)).

**Pick a jump-attack weapon** for the cancels. ***Nanobit*** notes the Dragon Scimitar *"doesn't have a plunge animation"* ([7:01, 5:07](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=307)).

## Where do Leggings of Lightness come from?

**Leggings of Lightness** come from the **Suspiciously Light Tattered Boots** vestige in a chest **atop the Temple Woods windmill** ([Leggings of Lightness, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Leggings_of_Lightness)). Since 1.0, Jagex *"replaced it with an ability that makes Windstep cost zero runes to cast while the Leggings are equipped"* ([1.0 notes, Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1374490/view/1843481262704158)). The trade is **zero leg armour**.

## Which tier of gear do you need?

**Power level 7 is the floor.** The wiki's Regicide page recommends *"Power level 7 or better armour and weapons"* ([Regicide, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Regicide)). On the Raid Lab Hardcore route that tier comes from **Umbral Sands** (Phase 5), where the Nightmare Crucibles' Ascension Cores lift your weapon from power level 6 to 7.

| Slot | Record route | Console route | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Melee** | Best mace, plus a shield for the Thane | Any melee weapon | ***SamFPlays***, ***FastTipsGaming*** |
| **Magic** | Best 2H mage staff | Fire magic weapon | ***SamFPlays***, ***FastTipsGaming*** |
| **Armour** | Best mage armour | Not stated | ***SamFPlays*** |
| **Second melee** | Dragon Scimitar (rib clip swing) | Not used | ***Nanobit*** |

**The Thane kill has a fixed rhythm.** ***SamFPlays***: *"Put on your mace, shield, and then you're going to do the air enchantment just so you attack faster."* ([Every Skip Explained, 5:38](https://youtu.be/ESrrkdxD1fo?t=338)). Enchant Weapon: Air sits at **Attack 30**.

**The Heart has two stances.** ***SamFPlays*** burns it with the staff from the third line: *"The third line here, just stand here, aim for the top of the heart. And left click."* ([10:40](https://youtu.be/ESrrkdxD1fo?t=640)). ***FastTipsGaming*** uses fire magic from range: *"While inside, my preferred method has always been fire magic."* ([Fight skip, 1:09](https://youtu.be/L-dTDXJyFq0?t=69)). ***Nanobit*** uses the **melee safespot**.

**The Dragon Scimitar** comes from your first clear: it *"can be received after completing the Scorned Wilderness raid and opening the Queenslayer's Reward Pack"* ([Dragon Scimitar, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Dragon_Scimitar)).

**Antipoison** is brewed from **Irit, Poison Ichor and a Glass Vessel** at a **Brewing Cauldron** ([Antipoison Potion, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Antipoison_Potion)). Carry one for the Heart on the hardcore-safe build.

**Nanobit's armour and food are a gap in the sources.** His quick-access slots hold his **helmet and pies**; his armour set is unstated, so match ***SamFPlays'*** mage armour.

## What this means when you run

- **Carry two Fleet-footed Infusions** and drink the second in the Stronghold.
- **Hold a mace for the Thane** and swap to the 2H staff for the Heart.
- **Wear a weapon with a plunge animation** for every dive cancel.

## Key takeaways

- The Fleet-footed Infusion halves sprint stamina cost for 300 seconds and is the one mandatory item.
- A mace kills the air Garou Thane, and a 2H mage staff with mage armour burns Kuldra's Heart.
- Strong Tea is the only rest restore inside the raid.
