**A skip drill** is **one skip, repeated in isolation**, with its **reference clip** looping beside you and **every attempt logged** as made or missed. The drill list below covers **every skip on the 1.0.1.2 route**, in split order, with the finder credited and the clip linked.

## How do you run a drill?

1. **Open the skip's reference clip** and watch it **twice at full speed**.
2. **Load into the split** that holds the skip.
3. **Attempt the skip**, then **log it**: made or missed.
4. **Repeat** for a fixed block of attempts.
5. **Move to the next weakest skip** when the block ends.

In the ***Raid Lab*** Practice page you **loop the clip** and log with **J** (made) and **K** (missed). It tracks **consistency per skip** and **suggests your weakest skip next** ([Raid Lab](https://drthomasager.github.io/dragonwilds-speedrun/)).

## Which skip do you drill first?

**Start with the movement tech.** ***Dotxy's*** **dive cancel** and **infinite double jump** feed the Sanctum flight, so drilling them on flat ground first makes the Sanctum drill short.

**Then drill the Fragments zoop.** ***Nanobit*** names it the precise one: *"You need to use it either on the frame you start walking off the rock... or 1 before."* ([comment on the 7:01](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=105)). He offers an easier fallback: *"if you want an easier (but slower) skip to Fragments I suggest checking out my Day 1 Strats Video"* ([8:32 run](https://youtu.be/vw_Mr5TGJAg?t=0)). **Drill the fallback until it is automatic**, then drill the fast version.

## How do you turn drills into runs?

**Use the timer and the race view.** The ***Raid Lab*** Timer behaves like LiveSplit, with **golds** and **PB**, and exports a `.lss` with the **WR**, ***Nanobit*** and the **community sum of best** as comparisons. It also **imports `.lss` history** to show **where your runs die**. **Race the WR** plays your run beside the 6:50 on one run clock.

**The run navigator** has **segment practice**: start the clock at any split, run that segment, and the result joins your history.

## What does a missed skip cost?

**Each miss has a price in seconds.** The Raid Lab model assigns a **time loss per miss** to every skip. Two of them are **measured** from the run path (the Keep puzzle and the Heart re-entry); the rest are **modelled estimates** of the recovery.

| Skip | Seconds lost per miss | What happens on a miss | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kuldra fight skip** | 48 | You fight Kuldra | Modelled |
| **Keep hole** | 44.3 | You solve the elemental puzzle | Measured puzzle cost + 6 s |
| **Garou Thane / air door** | 20 | Re-climb and re-charge air | Modelled |
| **Sanctum flight** | 15 | Drop out of the loop and climb again | Modelled |
| **Poison area** | 15 | Walk part of the poison area | Modelled |
| **Necro zoop to Fragments** | 12 | Walk the necro area edge | Modelled |
| **Black dragon** | 10 | Dragon aggro | Modelled |
| **Soulscourge, Abyssal Demon, parkour** | 8 each | Re-dash or re-climb | Modelled |
| **Heart standing spot** | 6 | Re-enter the safespot | Measured: ***Nanobit*** 7:01, 6:47 to 6:53 |

***Nanobit's*** own note is the Heart measurement: *"I slightly missed the melee safespot, I jump into it correctly at 6:53."* ([7:01, 6:47](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=407)).

## What make rate should you aim for?

**Aim for 90% or better on every skip, and drill the expensive ones first.** The Raid Lab practice model ranks drills by how much each one raises your chance of a sub-6:50. Its worked example uses a **synthetic new-runner profile** (generated practice logs, clearly labelled as not real data), so read the ranking as an **order of work** and set your own numbers from your own J/K logs.

| Priority | Drill | Synthetic make rate today | Target | Clean reps the model asks for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Keep hole | 52% | 76% | 27 |
| **2** | Sanctum flight | 36% | 68% | 22 |
| **3** | Garou Thane / air door | 71% | 86% | 43 |
| **4** | Necro zoop to Fragments | 69% | 84% | 32 |
| **5** | Black dragon | 82% | 91% | 17 |

**The targets halve the miss rate.** Every row in the model's plan is the same move: cut misses in half on one skip, then re-rank.

**Pace matters as much as make rate.** In the Raid Lab simulation (20,000 runs per route), the current-patch route with ***Dotxy's*** flight and ***Nanobit's*** Keep hole, run at ***TexasPete's*** calibrated pace, has a median of **7:31.95** and a best 1-in-1,000 run of **7:11.44**. The 6:48 run path closes that gap with **faster pieces**: ***TexasPete's*** Fragments line, ***FastTipsGaming's*** Thane launch and a flight about **3 s** faster than ***Dotxy's*** demo. **Drill those pieces for speed once your make rates sit above 90%.**

## What this means when you run

- **Drill the movement tech first**, then the Fragments zoop, then the rest in split order.
- **Log every attempt** with J and K so the weakest skip picks itself.
- **Import your `.lss` history** to find the split where your runs die.
