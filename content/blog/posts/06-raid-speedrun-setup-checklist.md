**The pre-run setup** is the **checklist you clear at Rasthin's Rest** before you talk to Commander Zilyana: **full rest**, **full hydration**, **bed spawn**, **keybinds**, **settings**, a **respawned Air Thane** and a **timer** that splits where speedrun.com does.

Every item on it **protects stamina, time or a split**.

## What is on the checklist?

| Check | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Rest** | 100, just slept | Low rest caps max stamina |
| **Fresh Start** | Active (180 s) | Rest, hydration and sustenance stop dwindling |
| **Hydration** | 100 | Fastest stamina regen |
| **Bed spawn** | Rasthin's Rest | Deaths return you to the start line |
| **Air Garou Thane** | Respawned | The Stronghold route kills him |
| **Ring keybinds** | Cancel spell slot 1, Surge 3, Phase Dash 4, Windstep 7 | Inputs inside the cancel window |
| **Backpack** | Two Fleet-footed Infusions, Strong Tea, mace, staff, runes | The loadout |
| **HUD and recording** | HUD visible, game audio on, recording started | speedrun.com video rules |
| **Timer** | Five splits, banner and cut-to-black triggers | Matches the speedrun.com rule |

## How do you set up the bed?

**The bed** needs **8 ash planks and 4 rough cloth**, and *"must be under a roof and enclosed with three walls to be used"* ([Bed, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Bed)). ***SamFPlays*** builds his right at the start: *"put a bed right here. Look at that janky roof, put a bed there. That way when you die you respawn right here."* ([Every Skip Explained, 1:43](https://youtu.be/ESrrkdxD1fo?t=103)).

## Why sleep right before the run?

**Rest caps your stamina.** The wiki's thresholds: **50 Tired** limits stamina to **80**, **25 Knackered** to **60**, **0 Exhausted** to **40** ([Rest, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Rest)). A full night's sleep also grants **Fresh Start**, a **180-second** buff that stops rest, hydration and sustenance dwindling.

**Sleep at Rasthin's Rest**, then start the run while Fresh Start covers the opening splits. Since 1.0.0.5, *"Summon Shelter will be able to be used in the Scorned Wilderness hub"* ([1.0.0.5 notes, Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1374490/view/1844751498218499)).

## Why drink to full hydration?

**Hydration sets stamina regen.** At **100 hydration** stamina regenerates at **6.0 per second**; at **0** it regenerates at **0.75** ([Hydration, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Hydration)). **Drink clean water** before entering; dirty water builds toward **Soulscourge**.

## Why check the Air Thane?

**The air Garou Thane** is a kill on the Stronghold route, and ***Nanobit*** found his respawn is slow: *"Air Thane takes like 15 minutes to respawn, it's best to just sleep through a night after completing a run... otherwise he doesn't spawn in the next run and ruins your time."* ([8:32 run, description](https://youtu.be/vw_Mr5TGJAg?t=0)).

**Sleep through a night between attempts.** One night covers **rest**, **Fresh Start** and **the Thane** together. Jagex's 1.0.1 notes add that *"Obstacles and Enemies now respawn in the Scorned Wildnerness Raid on reset/next run-through"* ([1.0.1 notes, Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1374490/view/1846651706673783)); **check the Thane is standing** in the air room before you commit to the gate kill.

## Which keybinds speed up the run?

**Every spell goes through the ring.** The wiki: players *"can access their customised spellbooks by pressing the Q-button (default)"* ([Magic, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Magic)). Pressing **Q then a number** picks a ring slot in one motion.

| Action | Key | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Spell ring** | `Q` (default) | Wiki Settings |
| **Cancel spell (Magical Mending)** | `Q` `1`, then cancel | ***Nanobit*** |
| **Surge** | `Q` `3` | ***loopuleasa*** |
| **Phase Dash** | `Q` `4` | ***loopuleasa*** |
| **Windstep** | `Q` `7` | ***Nanobit*** |
| **Jump / Double Jump** | `Space` (default) | Wiki Settings |
| **Evade, Air Dash, Surge charge** | `Ctrl` (default); ***SamFPlays*** uses `Alt` | Wiki Settings, ***SamFPlays*** |
| **Jump Attack** | Left-click above double-jump height | Wiki Settings |
| **Sprint** | `Shift` (default) | Wiki Settings |

***Nanobit***: *"I'm using Q1E for the plunge-cancel tech, and Q7 a lot of the time to windstep. However sometimes it's better to just cast normally if you're doing multiple casts, since you can just spam click to hit the next spell ASAP."* ([reply on the 7:01](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=0)). **Test what the final `E` does** in your own setup: ***Dotxy*** closes the ring with `Esc` instead.

**Number keys without the ring fire quick-access slots.** ***Nanobit***: *"I find spamming Q3/Q4 just equips my helmet and eats my pies when I don't want to, most of the time."* **Keep your quick-access slots clear of anything you mind triggering.**

**Hold sprint once.** ***Nanobit*** lost time at the start: *"Tapped sprint in beginning but was already sprinting, so walked in beginning for about 1.5s before re-sprinting"* ([7:01, 0:23](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=23)).

Console layouts are in the [console inputs post](/console-inputs-for-raid-tech/).

## Which settings should you test?

**No runner has published their settings.** Run each as a **test step**: change one, run ten attempts of the Fragments zoop, and log the make rate.

| Setting | Options | Test |
|---|---|---|
| **Frame Rate Limit** | Dynamic (default), 30, 60, 90, 120, 140 | Zoop distance at each cap |
| **KBM Action Direction** | Camera Facing or Character Facing | Which one sends Surge where you aim |
| **Sensitivity X / Y** | Default 5 | Your flight-loop camera control |
| **Visceral Attack Camera** | Toggle added in 1.0.1 | Camera stability during the Jump Attack |

**Framerate is the strongest lead.** ***Nanobit***: *"I think lower framerates can give better zoops as well, just from random observations, but I can't/haven't really tested that."* ([comment on the 7:01](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=105)). The option list comes from the wiki's Settings page: *"Frame Rate Limit: Dynamic (default), 30, 60, 90, 120, 140 frames per second"* ([Settings, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Settings)). **Lock the cap you choose** so every session compares cleanly.

## How should your timer and recording work?

**Match the speedrun.com rule.** The timer starts *"the first frame the loading icon disappears going into the raid"* and ends *"on the frame the screen goes black after the "Get out now!" timer ends after defeating Kuldra's Heart"* ([speedrun.com Raid rules](https://www.speedrun.com/Runescape_Dragonwilds)).

**Use the community five-split file**: Dragontail Vines, Fragments of Ghornfell, The Shattered Stronghold, Cataclysm Keep, Kuldra's Sanctum. **Target splits** for a 6:48 run are **1:22.70**, **2:23.20**, **3:15.70**, **4:42.30** and **6:48.10** (Raid Lab run path).

**Record full video with game audio** and keep the HUD visible: the game rules require *"a full video of the run, including game audio"* and state that *"Dynamic HUD elements should not be obscured or omitted (e.g. health, inventory, stamina)"*. **Start recording a few seconds early.** A moderator told ***ChiefWes***: *"make sure to start the recording a couple seconds sooner right before you go into the raid."* ([speedrun.com run](https://www.speedrun.com/Runescape_Dragonwilds/runs/z146l1wz)).

## What this means when you run

- **Sleep at Rasthin's Rest** before every attempt to refill rest, start Fresh Start and respawn the air Garou Thane.
- **Bind the ring the way the runners do**: cancel spell on 1, Surge on 3, Phase Dash on 4, Windstep on 7.
- **Lock your framerate cap and start recording early** with game audio and the HUD visible.
