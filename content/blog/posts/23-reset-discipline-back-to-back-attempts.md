**Reset discipline** is the set of habits that put the **raid and your character back to a clean start** between attempts: the **air Garou Thane respawned**, **rest and hydration full**, **infusions stocked** and the **known hazards on repeat runs** handled.

**A clean reset protects the next attempt's splits.**

## What resets between attempts?

| Item | How it resets | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Obstacles and enemies** | Respawn on reset or next run-through | Jagex 1.0.1 notes |
| **AI on the second attempt** | Respawns as intended | Jagex 1.0.0.5 notes |
| **Air Garou Thane** | About 15 minutes to respawn | ***Nanobit*** |
| **Rest** | Full night's sleep refills it | Wiki |
| **Respawn points** | Deaths no longer skip a respawn point | Jagex 1.0.0.5 notes |

## Why sleep between attempts?

**The air Garou Thane respawns slowly.** ***Nanobit***, on the day-one 1.0.1 route: *"Air Thane takes like 15 minutes to respawn, it's best to just sleep through a night after completing a run... otherwise he doesn't spawn in the next run and ruins your time."* ([8:32 run, description](https://youtu.be/vw_Mr5TGJAg?t=0)).

**The 1.0.1 notes cover the rest of the raid.** Jagex: *"Obstacles and Enemies now respawn in the Scorned Wildnerness Raid on reset/next run-through."* ([1.0.1 notes, Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1374490/view/1846651706673783)). **Sleep through a night**, then **confirm the Thane is standing** in the air room as your first Stronghold check.

**The same night refills rest.** A night's sleep is a **full refill** and grants **Fresh Start**; each death costs **15 rest** ([Rest, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Rest)).

## What is the Commander teleport drop?

**The Commander teleport drop** is a **repeat-run hazard** at Kuldra: Commander Zilyana's teleport places you inside the ground and you fall. ***Bazilleos*** recorded it: *"Happens every time you repeat final boss. Commander teleports you to the next platform and in doing so you spawn in middle of the ground and fall down to the death"* ([Kuldra Boss new bug](https://youtu.be/JuuIF0BeGaE?t=0)). ***Vytautas Balnis*** filmed the same drop on 23 October ([clip](https://youtu.be/zl7R_N-vByw?t=0)).

**Plan around it.** **Reactive Recall** at Agility 70 turns a fatal fall into a teleport back to your marker ([Reactive Recall, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Reactive_Recall)), and **a fresh session** after a night's sleep is the cleanest state to test from.

## How do you restock between attempts?

1. **Drink a fresh Fleet-footed Infusion** at the start: its Fleet Footed buff lasts **300 seconds**.
2. **Carry a second infusion** for the Stronghold. ***Nanobit***: *"Don't forget to re-use your fleet-footed infusion here, because I did."* ([7:01, 3:22](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=202)).
3. **Refill Strong Tea, runes and food**, then run the [pre-run checklist](/raid-speedrun-setup-checklist/).

## When should you reset a run?

**Reset the moment a split falls behind its threshold.** The Raid Lab simulation searched for the reset points that give the most sub-6:50 runs per hour, assuming **45 seconds** from reset to the next timer start. For a runner at ***TexasPete's*** calibrated pace on his own line, the thresholds are:

| Split | Reset if you are slower than | 6:48 target split |
|---|---|---|
| **Dragontail Vines** | 1:22.50 | 1:22.70 |
| **Fragments of Ghornfell** | 2:24.75 | 2:23.20 |
| **The Shattered Stronghold** | 3:16.00 | 3:15.70 |
| **Cataclysm Keep** | 4:52.75 | 4:42.30 |

**Resetting doubles the rate of record-pace runs in the model.** With these thresholds the simulation finds **0.18 sub-6:50 runs per hour** (one every **5.6 hours**); finishing every attempt gives **0.08 per hour**. These are **modelled** numbers from a 20,000-run simulation, calibrated so the 6:50 is the best of about 177 attempts. **Use your own target splits** from the [run path](/scorned-wilderness-world-record-data/) as the reset line once you have a PB.

## How do you reset fastest on PC?

**Force-close after a failed early skip.** A reply under ***Nanobit's*** 7:01: *"If you force close your game process after failing the zoop, you can reset raid progress to not-started to get more attempts without having to complete the entire raid every time. ... I recommend backing up your character json if you do this, just in-case."* ([reply on the 7:01](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=105)). **Back up your character file** before your first force-close.

## What else drops you between attempts?

**Autosaves interrupt the flight loop.** ***FastTipsGaming***: *"If the game auto saves while you're trying to perform this trick, you're going down."* ([Infinite Jump, 0:57](https://youtu.be/U0_KPXu28qw?t=57)). **Log autosave drops separately** from skill misses so your make rate stays honest.

**After a full clear the raid resets to the start.** The wiki's quick guide: *"Once Kuldra has been slain, Commander Zilyana's ability to teleport you to locations other than Dragontail Vines will reset."* ([Quick guide, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Scorned_Wilderness/Quick_guide)).

## What this means when you run

- **Sleep through a night** after every completed attempt so the air Garou Thane is back.
- **Check the Thane is standing** before you commit to the gate kill.
- **Reset at the split** the moment you fall behind its threshold.
