**Raid tech inputs** are the **button layouts** that let you fire ***Dotxy's*** cancel loop and the Surge and dash chains **fast enough** to gain height. On console the loop runs through a **spell radial**; on PC it runs through **Q plus a number key**.

**The loop is learnable on console.** ***IINo1goodII***: *"I've been doing this consistently on console. btw i'm sure you know you can dash n phase dash doing this too."* ([comment on Sanctum flight](https://youtu.be/GeWbo4GpFEg?t=0)). ***FastTipsGaming*** completed a **full unedited raid and boss skip** on the current patch with it on 2 November ([full run](https://youtu.be/oK0_U_I40DY?t=0)).

## What is the loop you are mapping?

| Step | Input | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Windstep** | Starts the climb; cast once |
| **2** | **Jump Attack** | Enters the plunge animation |
| **3** | **Q (Spell Ring)** | Opens the ring |
| **4** | **Any targeted AoE spell** | The cast you cancel |
| **5** | **Escape** | Closes the ring, cancels the cast |
| **6** | **Jump** | Refunded air jump |

***Dotxy***: *"Repeat steps 2-6 as needed!"* ([Sanctum flight, description](https://youtu.be/GeWbo4GpFEg?t=0)). Speed matters: *"If you're faster than I am you can gain height, not just hover lol"* ([Infinite Double Jump](https://youtu.be/goKfbT19nkk?t=0)).

## What do you need for it?

***FastTipsGaming***: *"The only thing you need is access to a melee weapon, two spells, and enough runes to cast them."* ([Infinite Jump, 0:06](https://youtu.be/U0_KPXu28qw?t=6)). The two spells are **Windstep** and the cancel spell, and he names the default: *"Everyone pretty much agrees that magical mending is the best one to use because most people unlock it earlier than the others."* ([0:17](https://youtu.be/U0_KPXu28qw?t=17)).

## How do you set it up on PlayStation?

***FastTipsGaming's*** layout:

1. **Clear one spell radial**: *"clear some room in one of your spell radials and place only two spells, wind step and magic mending."* ([1:06](https://youtu.be/U0_KPXu28qw?t=66))
2. **Windstep** on **Triangle + L1**, then **X** to jump.
3. At the height of the jump, **R1** for the slam (Jump Attack).
4. **Triangle** at the very start of the slam, then **L2** to cast **Magical Mending**, which cancels the slam.
5. **L2 again** to cancel the spell.
6. **X** to jump, and repeat from step 3.

In his words: *"press triangle then L1. This simply gives you wind step. Then press X to jump ... at the height of your jump, press R1 to do a slam attack. ... press triangle in the very beginning of our slam attack ... Press L2 to activate the spell magical mending, which also cancels out your slam attack. ... After activating magical mending, press L2 again to cancel the spell. ... simply press X again to jump."* ([1:35](https://youtu.be/U0_KPXu28qw?t=95))

| Action | FastTipsGaming (PS) | brendonboyd7668 (PS5) |
|---|---|---|
| **Windstep** | Triangle + L1 | Triangle + R1 |
| **Cancel spell** | Triangle + L2 (Magical Mending) | Triangle + R2, or L2 left-handed (repair equipment spell) |
| **Slam** | R1 at jump height | R1 |
| **Jump** | X | X |

***brendonboyd7668***: *"Set your spell wheel to where wind step is Triangle R1 input. Set your repair equipment spell to Traingle R2 for Righties or L2 for lefties."* and *"You don't need to re input windstep after the first time just keep the pattern from Step 2."* ([comment on Sanctum flight](https://youtu.be/GeWbo4GpFEg?t=0)).

**Xbox players** use the same positions on the matching Xbox buttons; no Xbox runner has published a layout yet, so **test your mapping** on flat ground first.

## How do you set it up on PC?

**Use Q plus a number key.** ***Nanobit***: *"I'm using Q1E for the plunge-cancel tech, and Q7 a lot of the time to windstep."* ([reply on the 7:01](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=0)). ***loopuleasa***: *"I spam Q+3 and Q+4 to enable surge and dash"* ([comment on the 7:01](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=0)). The full PC table is in the [setup checklist](/raid-speedrun-setup-checklist/).

## Can you use a macro?

**Ask the moderators first.** ***Dotxy*** suggests one: *"Feel free to set up a macro for easy infinite flight!"* ([Sanctum flight](https://youtu.be/GeWbo4GpFEg?t=0)). The speedrun.com Raid rules cover **timing** and the game rules cover **video** and **good faith**: *"Runs are expected to be performed in good faith and may be rejected based on mod discretion if performed otherwise."* ([speedrun.com](https://www.speedrun.com/Runescape_Dragonwilds)). **Message the moderators, Archy and dotyoureyes**, and get a written answer before you submit a macro run.

## How do you build the rhythm?

1. **Drill the cancel on flat ground**: slam, cast, cancel, jump.
2. **Count the loop out loud** until it is even.
3. **Add Windstep** and aim to **gain height** each cycle.
4. **Mix in a dash or Phase Dash**, as ***IINo1goodII*** describes.
5. **Watch for autosaves.** ***FastTipsGaming***: *"If the game auto saves while you're trying to perform this trick, you're going down."* ([0:57](https://youtu.be/U0_KPXu28qw?t=57))
6. **Move to the Sanctum** once you climb on most loops.

## What this means when you run

- **Put only Windstep and Magical Mending on one radial** so the loop runs with one hand shape.
- **Drill the cancel rhythm on flat ground** before you add Windstep.
- **Get a written moderator answer** before you submit a run that uses a macro.
