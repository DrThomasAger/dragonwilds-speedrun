The **infinite double jump** is ***Dotxy's*** loop of **dive cancels and air jumps** that keeps you airborne for as long as you hold the rhythm; chained through Kuldra's Sanctum it is called **Sanctum flight**. It is the route's answer to patch 1.0.1.2.

**This is the swing segment of the record.** Both record runs climbed the blocks from the Sanctum door to the heart tunnel in **35.0 s**, a route 1.0.1.2 closed. ***Dotxy's*** flight demo covers the same stretch in **51.9 s**, and the 6:48.10 target pace asks for **49.0 s**. Every second of flight you save goes straight off the run.

## Why does the route need flight?

**Patch 1.0.1.2 closed the old way up.** ***Nanobit***: *"Windstepping up the blocks to Kuldra no longer works as of 1.0.1.2/1.0.1.3. You can still get up to that area via just flying lmao."* ([7:01 description](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=0))

***Dotxy* posted flight the same day as the patch:** *"here's a new way to get through Kuldra's Sanctum quick in the Runescape Dragonwilds 1.0.1.2 hotfix!"* ([Sanctum flight](https://youtu.be/GeWbo4GpFEg?t=0))

## What do you need to fly?

| Requirement | Unlock | Role in the loop |
|---|---|---|
| **Windstep** | Agility 5 | Starts the loop once |
| **Double Jump** | Agility 10 | The jump at step 6 |
| **Death From Above!** | Attack 25 perk | The Jump Attack at step 2 |
| **Targeted AoE spell** | spell ring | The cast at step 4 |
| **Dive cancel** | technique | [Learn it first](/dive-cancel-jump-attack-cancel/) |

## How do you do the loop?

***Dotxy's*** inputs, verbatim from his description:

1. **Windstep**
2. **Jump Attack**
3. **Q (Spell Ring)**
4. **[any targeted aoe spell]**
5. **Escape** (closes Spell Ring interface)
6. **Jump**

*"Repeat steps 2-6 as needed!"* Windstep starts the loop once; steps 2 to 6 repeat.

**Speed gains height.** ***Dotxy***: *"If you're faster than I am you can gain height, not just hover lol"* ([Infinite Double Jump](https://youtu.be/goKfbT19nkk?t=0)).

**Dashes mix in.** ***IINo1goodII***: *"btw i'm sure you know you can dash n phase dash doing this too."* ([comment](https://youtu.be/GeWbo4GpFEg?t=0))

Load the [90-second flight](https://youtu.be/GeWbo4GpFEg?t=0) **now** and run the loop on flat ground until it hovers.

## How do you do it on console?

**It works on console.** ***IINo1goodII***: *"I've been doing this consistently on console."* ***brendonboyd7668*** shares a PS5 layout:

1. *"Set your spell wheel to where wind step is Triangle R1 input. Set your repair equipment spell to Traingle R2 for Righties or L2 for lefties."*
2. *"Start off by practicing this pattern Repair Spell, Cancel spell, double jump R1"*
3. *"Add wind step to the beginning of the pattern"*

*"You don't need to re input windstep after the first time just keep the pattern from Step 2."* ([comment](https://youtu.be/GeWbo4GpFEg?t=0))

## Can you use a macro?

**Ask the moderators first.** ***Dotxy*** suggests one: *"Feel free to set up a macro for easy infinite flight!"* The speedrun.com raid rules state the timing and video requirements and are silent on macros. **Message *Archy* or *dotyoureyes* before you submit a run that uses one.**

## Does it still work today?

**Yes, as of 2 November 2026.** ***FastTipsGaming*** posted *"my full unedited Kuldra raid and boss skip in Runescape Dragonwilds"* ([full raid](https://youtu.be/oK0_U_I40DY?t=0)) using the infinite-jump cycle, plus a tutorial ([Infinite Jump EXPLOIT](https://youtu.be/U0_KPXu28qw?t=0)).

## What this means when you run

- **Drill steps 2 to 6** on flat ground until you climb instead of hover.
- **Fly the Sanctum in practice** and race the door-to-tunnel stretch against the 49.0 s target.
- **Confirm the macro ruling** with the moderators before any submitted run.
