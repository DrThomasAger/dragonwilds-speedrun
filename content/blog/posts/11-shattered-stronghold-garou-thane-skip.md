**The Garou Thane skip** is the route through **The Shattered Stronghold** that skips the Garou Thane area. The proven 1.0.1 version has you **kill the air-attuned Garou Thane through the closed gate** with a melee weapon, take his **air charge**, **windjump** *(a Windstep jump)* up to the **air door** and leave through a hole in the wall. ***Sir_Rebral*** found the gate kill, and ***SamFPlays*** credits him for it.

***SamFPlays*** calls this **"the HARDEST part of the raid"**. It is the **third split**, and it is ***TexasPete's*** biggest lead: **0:51.70** against ***Nanobit's*** **1:03.10**.

**A faster option arrived on 2 November.** ***FastTipsGaming*** skips the Thane kill entirely: he climbs the rocks left of the air door, Windsteps and glides over the island into the forest in about **51.9 s**. That ties ***TexasPete's*** patched climb, so this post teaches the **launch first** and ***Sir_Rebral's*** **Thane kill as the fallback**.

## What do you need?

| Requirement | Unlock | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Best mace you have** | Melee weapon | Killing the Thane through the gate |
| **Windstep** | Agility 5 (infobox shows 8) | The windjump to the air door |
| **Air charge** | Drops from killing the air Thane | Opening the air door |
| **Fleet-footed Infusion** | Re-drink in this split | Sprinting through the Keep |

**A Garou Thane** is a Garou with a tower shield that blocks every attack until its stance breaks. **Crush** is its weakness, which makes a mace the right tool. See [What gear do you bring?](/raid-speedrun-gear-and-consumables/)

## How do you do the no-kill launch?

**The no-kill launch** is ***FastTipsGaming's*** route from his full unedited run of 2 November, played after 1.0.1.2. It needs **no air charge** and **no mace swing**.

1. **Climb the rocks to the left of the air door** ([FastTipsGaming, 4:43](https://youtu.be/oK0_U_I40DY?t=283)).
2. **Windstep from the top** ([4:59](https://youtu.be/oK0_U_I40DY?t=299)).
3. **Glide over the floating island** into the forest ([5:00 to 5:19](https://youtu.be/oK0_U_I40DY?t=300)).
4. **Carry on into the parkour** toward Cataclysm Keep.

**This is a test step.** The 51.9 s comes from a tutorial-pace video with no on-screen timer (split point estimated, ±1.5 s). Land it three times on 1.0.1.2, then route it.

## How do you do the Thane kill?

**The Thane kill is the fallback**, proven in the 7:01. These are ***Nanobit's*** day-one steps, matched to ***SamFPlays'*** chapter:

1. **Pass the water door** into the Stronghold.
2. **Kill the air-anima Garou Thane through the closed gate** with your mace. ***Nanobit*** notes the Thane *"likes to walk through the closed gate on his own"*.
3. **Air-charged, windjump from the tip of the rubble pile** up to the **air door**, and go through.
4. **Exit through the hole in the wall** above the **orb dispenser**.
5. **Re-drink your Fleet-footed Infusion** here. ***Nanobit*** names this exact moment.
6. **Zoop to the Keep** for distance. ***Nanobit***: a strong one needs **zero dashes** and places you at his 3:57 spot.

**Stop swinging the moment the Thane dies.** ***Sir_Rebral*** watched ***Nanobit's*** extra hit after the kill cost the 7:01.

## How do you reset between attempts?

**The air Thane respawns slowly.** On 1.0.1 day one ***Nanobit*** timed it at about **15 minutes**, so he **sleeps through a night** after every run. Jagex's 1.0.1 notes say enemies now respawn on reset: check the Thane is standing before you start a timed attempt. The full reset routine is in [How do you reset between attempts?](/reset-discipline-back-to-back-attempts/)

## How fast is each Stronghold route?

**The segment runs from the split 2 portal to the split 3 banner in the forest** (about ±0.3 s for timed runs).

| Route | Run | Time (s) | Status on 1.0.1.2 | Clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wall climb with no Thane kill | ***TexasPete*** 6:50 | 51.7 | Patched (1.0.1 runners need the air charge) | [6:50, 2:30](https://youtu.be/6WEIHo7AUf0?t=150) |
| No-kill launch over the island | ***FastTipsGaming*** | **51.9** | Test step (no timer, ±1.5 s) | [4:41](https://youtu.be/oK0_U_I40DY?t=281) |
| Wall climb with no Thane kill | ***TexasPete*** 7:40 | 54.4 | Patched | [7:40, 3:18](https://youtu.be/6N3NwYA5Zh0?t=198) |

| Guide route through the air room | ***SamFPlays*** 10:53 | 100.3 | Works | [follow-along, 3:32](https://youtu.be/WpvNjqVmpVM?t=212) |

**TexasPete's 11.4 s lead over the 7:01 comes from the wall climb**, which skips the Thane fight and is now patched. ***Nanobit*** names where the 7:01 gives time back: the extra swing, and the 3:46 and 3:50 zoops, worth about **6 s** together by his notes.

**The target is 52.5 s**: the no-kill launch plus a 0.6 s margin. The **fallback** is the Thane kill at about **57 s** with the stronger zoops. The **split 3 target is 3:15.70**. Against ***SamFPlays'*** guide route, the launch saves **48.4 s** and the Thane kill saves **37.2 s**.

## What this means when you run

- **Test FastTipsGaming's no-kill launch** three times on 1.0.1.2 and route it when it lands.
- **Keep the Thane kill ready** as the fallback: best mace, exact swings, windjump from the rubble tip.
- **Re-drink Fleet-footed Infusion** before you leave the Stronghold.
