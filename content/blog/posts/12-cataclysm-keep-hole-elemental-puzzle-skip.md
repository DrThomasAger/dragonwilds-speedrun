**The Cataclysm Keep skip** is a **hole in the Keep** that drops you **out of bounds** *(outside the playable map)* past the **elemental puzzle** *(four pressure pads stepped in the order Air, Water, Earth, Fire)*. ***IINo1goodII*** and ***notmusty1*** found it on patch 1.0.1, and ***Nanobit*** used it in his 7:01, thanking them for *"a lovely hole in Keep"*.

Cataclysm Keep is the **fourth split**, and it is where ***Nanobit*** wins: **1:27.80** against ***TexasPete's*** **1:36.30**.

## Who found it?

**The credit chain runs through both finders.** ***Nanobit*** credits *"@notmusty1 via @IINo1goodII"*, and ***SamFPlays*** titles his chapter *"thank you @IINo1goodII and @notmusty1"*. Both posted their own videos of the skip on 16 October.

| Requirement | Unlock | Used for |

| **Phase Dash** | Agility 44 | Saved for Kuldra when the zoop is strong |
| **Second weapon with a plunge** | Any weapon except the Dragon Scimitar | Plunge cancels |

## How do you do the parkour?

***SamFPlays*** calls this stretch **"Parkour section made easy"**. Follow his line from the [follow-along](https://youtu.be/WpvNjqVmpVM?t=320), then add the record details below.

## How do you do the Keep hole?

1. **Jump into the out-of-bounds area** from ***Nanobit's*** reference point ([7:01, 4:38](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=278)).
2. **Land on the first rock** and keep moving; the extra jump onto the rock at 4:44 is optional.
3. **Plunge cancel** at 5:07 and 5:12 with a weapon that has a **plunge animation**. ***Nanobit*** reminds you the Dragon Scimitar has none.
4. **Use the cancel to dash onto the first rock**, then zoop.
5. **Save the Phase Dash for Kuldra** when the zoop is strong enough to need only one dash.

## What is the backup when the hole fails?

**Solve the puzzle fast.** ***Sir_Rebral's*** advice from before the hole was found still holds: *"just solve the puzzle fast with smart pathing."* The pads go **Air, Water, Earth, Fire**, and ***SuperStooob's*** guide shows the solution ([14:33](https://youtu.be/7s3fgHwlRYA?t=873)).

## How fast is the Keep hole?

**The Keep is timed in two pieces** (about ±0.3 s): the parkour from the split 3 banner to the Keep entry, past the last fire beam, and the run from the Keep entry to the **Sanctum door load**. The second piece includes the rib clip, taught in the [Sanctum post](/kuldras-sanctum-flight-and-fight-skip/).

| Segment | ***TexasPete*** 6:50 | ***Nanobit*** 7:01 | ***TexasPete*** 7:40 | ***SamFPlays*** 10:53 | ***FastTipsGaming*** | Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Parkour to Keep entry | 35.3 | **33.6** | 39.3 | 42.7 | | 33.6 |
| Keep entry to Sanctum door | 83.8 (old skip, patched) | **75.2** (hole) | 90.0 (old skip, patched) | 143.0 (full puzzle) | 98.6 (hole, tutorial pace) | 73.5 |

**Nanobit's 8.5 s lead in this split comes from the hole.** The 1.0.1 hole beats ***TexasPete's*** old Keep skip *(a blue rock climb, patched in 1.0.1)* by **8.6 s** door to door, and it beats the full elemental puzzle by **67.8 s**, the single biggest save in the route.

**The target is 73.5 s**: ***Nanobit's*** 75.2 s minus the plunge cancels at 5:07 and 5:12, the spare rock jump at 4:44 and an instant rib clip (at least 3 s). The **split 4 target is 4:42.30**, about 8 s ahead of the 6:50.

## What this means when you run

- **Jump into the hole from Nanobit's 4:38 reference point.**
- **Hold a weapon with a plunge animation** for the cancels at the end of the Keep.
- **Practise the Air, Water, Earth, Fire puzzle** as your backup for a run where the hole fails.
