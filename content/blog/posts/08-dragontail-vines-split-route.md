**Dragontail Vines** is the **first split** of the Scorned Wilderness raid, from the loading screen to the first **extraction point**. The 1.0.1.2 route clears it with **three skips** in a row: the **Windstep to the first air anima**, the **soulscourge skip** and the **black dragon skip**, ending on the jump-off point for the zoop to Fragments.

This is where ***TexasPete*** builds his lead. His Vines split is **1:21.52**, against ***Nanobit's*** **1:27.82**, so every dash you save here carries all the way to the cut to black.

## What do you need?

| Requirement | Unlock | Used for |

| **Windstep** | Agility 5 (infobox shows 8) | Launch to the first air anima |
| **Double Jump** | Agility 10 | Height on every jump |
| **Air Dash** | Agility 32 | Four dashes to the first pillar |
| **Phase Dash** | Agility 44 | The soulscourge skip |
| **Fleet-footed Infusion** | Cooking range: 1 Clean Water + 1 Feathers | Half-cost sprinting for 300 s |

## How do you do the air anima Windstep?

**Crystallised Anima** is raid scenery you touch to **attune to an element** for 30 seconds. ***SamFPlays*** routes the **air charge early** so it opens the way ahead.

1. **Sprint from the first frame.** ***Nanobit*** walked for a moment here and names the cost: about 1.5 s.
2. **Windstep toward the first air anima pillar** and chain **four air dashes** on the way.
3. **Take three air dashes** toward the **second air anima pillar**.

***zyneater*** adds a faster line in his guide description: a better route *"directly to the first air anima vent"*. Watch his [0 to 1 tips](https://youtu.be/1Q21XYSKJgo?t=239) and build that line into your practice.

## How do you do the soulscourge skip?

***SamFPlays*** names it the **"Soulscourge skip (phase dash + food trick)"**. It carries you past the soulscourge area on a **Phase Dash** chain.

1. **Follow his line** from the [explained chapter](https://youtu.be/ESrrkdxD1fo?t=151).
2. **Spend the Phase Dash** where he does, and copy his food timing from the [original chapter](https://youtu.be/iu2SL4oyZFQ?t=40).
3. **I-frame Kuldra's first hit** *(i-frames: the invulnerable moment of a dash)* with a well-timed dash and a **tight line over the torch** into the first black dragon area, as ***Nanobit*** describes.

## How do you do the black dragon skip?

The route runs **straight past the first black dragon** *(a power level 8 dragon that every route skips)*.

1. **Start the Earth Pillar early.** ***Nanobit*** measures about 1 s here.
2. **Take the clean line down** after the dragon to the **jump-off point**.
3. **Set up the zoop to Fragments**, covered in [How do you skip to Fragments of Ghornfell?](/skip-to-fragments-of-ghornfell/)

## How fast is each Vines skip?

**Each skip is timed checkpoint to checkpoint** in four runs (about ±0.3 s). The **target** is the pace that beats the record, built from the best valid piece plus ***Nanobit's*** time saves.

| Segment | ***TexasPete*** 6:50 | ***Nanobit*** 7:01 | ***TexasPete*** 7:40 | ***SamFPlays*** 10:53 | Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start to first air anima | **20.5** | 22.1 | 28.6 | 27.9 | 21.0 |
| Air anima to soulscourge passed | 39.9 | **39.3** | 41.4 | 51.5 | 39.3 |
| Black dragon arena | 11.6 | 10.8 | **10.4** | 14.6 | 10.4 |

| Best piece | Evidence | Clip |

**The split 1 target is 1:22.70.** The last piece of the split, from the black dragon to Fragments, is timed in [How do you skip to Fragments of Ghornfell?](/skip-to-fragments-of-ghornfell/)

## What this means when you run

- **Count your dashes**: four to the first air anima pillar, three to the second.
- **Dash through Kuldra's first hit** so the knockback costs you nothing.
- **Arrive at the jump-off point with stamina** for the zoop to Fragments.
