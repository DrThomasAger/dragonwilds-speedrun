***The 6:50*** is ***TexasPete's*** **6:50.000** run of the Scorned Wilderness raid, the **number one time** on the speedrun.com Raid board (version 1.0.0.6+). You beat it by combining ***Nanobit's*** **1.0.1 route** with ***Dotxy's*** **1.0.1.2 flight** and playing the five splits at the **community-best pace**.

This series teaches every step, in the order you need it. It is **a route you can run with**.

## Is the 6:50 beatable today?

**The 6:50 is beatable on today's patch.** ***TexasPete*** played the record on 14 October, *"prior to the windstep double jump and some other new routing strats"* ([6:50 description](https://youtu.be/6WEIHo7AUf0?t=0)). Since then ***Dotxy*** has found a way to **fly through Kuldra's Sanctum**, and ***FastTipsGaming*** has shown a Stronghold launch with no Thane kill. Built only from pieces that still work on 1.0.1.2, the frame-timed run path adds up to **6:52.80**, and the **target pace is 6:48.10**, with the extra seconds coming from the Sanctum flight, the heart safespot and the Keep.

***Nanobit*** gives the verdict on the current route: *"clearly can be done sub 7 judging by my sum of best, but needs very clean movement and strong zoops"* ([7:01 description](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=0)).

## What is the Scorned Wilderness raid speedrun?

***The Scorned Wilderness raid*** is the end-game raid of RuneScape: Dragonwilds *(a chain of floating islands that ends at Kuldra, the God-Eater)*. The speedrun.com **Raid** category times it from the loading screen to the escape:

> "Timer starts the first frame the loading icon disappears going into the raid. Timer ends on the frame the screen goes black after the "Get out now!" timer ends after defeating Kuldra's Heart." ([speedrun.com rules](https://www.speedrun.com/Runescape_Dragonwilds))

The run has **five splits**, each ending at an extraction point:

| Split | Area | TexasPete 6:50 | Nanobit 7:01 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dragontail Vines** | 81.52 s | 87.82 s |
| 2 | **Fragments of Ghornfell** | 61.10 s | 67.10 s |
| 3 | **The Shattered Stronghold** | 51.70 s | 63.10 s |
| 4 | **Cataclysm Keep** | 96.30 s | 87.80 s |
| 5 | **Kuldra's Sanctum** | 119.38 s | 116.15 s |

Segment times are measured frame by frame from each video (±0.1 s), as explained in **[Where is the 6:50 won?](/scorned-wilderness-world-record-data/)**.

## What this means when you run

- Learn the **movement kit first**; every skip on today's route is built from five primitives.
- Run to the **split targets 1:22.70, 2:23.20, 3:15.70, 4:42.30 and 6:48.10**: level with the 6:50 through split 3, about 8 s ahead after the Keep.
- Spend your practice hours in **the Sanctum**, the swing segment: bring Dotxy's 51.9 s flight down to the 49.0 s target.

## Key takeaways

- The 6:50 was played on a route that patch 1.0.1 has since changed, so today's record attempt runs a newer route.
- The 1.0.1.2-valid run path adds up to 6:52.80, and the 6:48.10 target takes about 4.7 s more from the Sanctum, the heart and the Keep.
- The Sanctum decides the record: flight costs about 17 s over the patched blocks, so a sub-6:50 banks about 8 s by the Keep exit and spends it there.
