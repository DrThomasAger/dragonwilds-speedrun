**The skip to Fragments of Ghornfell** is a **zoop** *(a long launch off an edge)* that carries you from the end of Dragontail Vines straight into Fragments, **skipping the whole necromancer area**. ***SectorTSWG*** found it on patch 1.0.1, ***Sir_Rebral*** spread it, and ***Nanobit*** credits both in his 7:01.

It closes **split 1** and it comes in **two versions**: a **frame-tight zoop** the records use, and an **easier zoop into a respawn** that ***Nanobit*** ran on 1.0.1 day one.

## Who found it?

***Sir_Rebral*** writes in his skip video that he learned it from ***SectorTSWG***, and ***Nanobit*** thanks *"@SectorTSWG via @Sir_Rebral for the new skip to Fragments"*. ***SectorTSWG's*** own run went up on the day 1.0.1 launched, with the note that he *"had to find a couple of work arounds"*.

## What do you need?

| Requirement | Unlock | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Double Jump** | Agility 10 | Height off the rock |
| **Air Dash** | Agility 32 | Stretching a short zoop |
| **Phase Dash** | Agility 44 | Recovering a weak zoop |
| **Stamina** | Fleet-footed Infusion active | Dashes at the end of the split |

See [Which Agility levels do you need?](/agility-unlocks-for-raid-speedruns/) and [What is a zoop?](/what-is-a-zoop/)

## How do you do the fast version?

**The fast version** is the one in the 7:01. ***Nanobit*** calls it *"relatively precise"* and says half his runs died here, so build it in practice first.

1. **Walk or run to the end of the rock** at the jump-off point.
2. **Zoop on the frame you start walking off the rock**, or the frame before. ***Nanobit*** explains the reason: the rock has *"an incredibly small slope after the flat part, before it drops off completely"*.
3. **Fly straight into Fragments** and continue.

**Walking gives you more time to react.** ***Nanobit*** found no distance difference between walking and running.

## How do you do the easier version?

**The easier version** lands you in a **respawn** past the necromancer area. These are ***Nanobit's*** day-one steps, as written in the route:

1. **Zoop across to necro land.**
2. **Zoop off the rocks left of the bridge** toward the **top of the left pillar**. It needs height; use one or both dashes depending on the strength of the zoop.
3. **Respawn** (the zoop causes it) and continue.

## How fast is each version?

**The segment runs from leaving the black dragon arena to the split 1 banner**, timed in four runs (about ±0.3 s).

| Version | Run | Time (s) | Status on 1.0.1.2 | Clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Direct line off the dragon ledge | ***TexasPete*** 6:50 | **9.5** | Test step | [6:50, 1:19](https://youtu.be/6WEIHo7AUf0?t=79) |
| Direct line off the dragon ledge | ***TexasPete*** 7:40 | 9.5 | Test step | [7:40, 1:50](https://youtu.be/6N3NwYA5Zh0?t=110) |
| Vines zoop to Fragments | ***Nanobit*** 7:01 | 15.6 | Works | [7:01, 1:36](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=96) |
| Death at necro island, respawn at Fragments | ***SamFPlays*** 10:53 | 21.2 | Works | [follow-along, 1:33](https://youtu.be/WpvNjqVmpVM?t=93) |

**The target is 12.0 s**: ***TexasPete's*** 9.5 s line plus a 1.5 s margin. The **fallback** is ***Nanobit's*** zoop started about 1.5 s earlier, around **14.1 s**, which costs about 2 s against the target.

**The zoop beats the respawn by 24.5 s** from the dragon to the fortress courtyard (41.6 s against 66.1 s), because the respawn adds the *YOU DIED* screen and the wait.

## Does TexasPete's direct line still work?

**This is a test step.** ***TexasPete*** leaves the black dragon ledge and goes **straight across to Fragments** in 9.5 s, a 1.0.0.6-era line that no 1.0.1 runner uses on video yet.

1. **Load his line** at [6:50, 1:19](https://youtu.be/6WEIHo7AUf0?t=79) and copy the take-off point.
2. **Try it three times on 1.0.1.2.**
3. **Route it when it lands**, and keep ***Nanobit's*** zoop as the fallback when it fails.

## What this means when you run

- **Learn the respawn version first**, then switch to the fast zoop once you land it every time in practice.
- **Zoop on the last flat frame** of the rock, or the frame before.
- **Test TexasPete's 9.5 s direct line** on 1.0.1.2 and keep the zoop as your fallback.

## Key takeaways

- The skip to Fragments is a zoop that bypasses the necromancer area, found by SectorTSWG and spread by Sir_Rebral.
- The fast version needs the zoop on the last flat frame of the rock, or the frame before.
