**Agility** is the skill that owns the **movement kit** in RuneScape: Dragonwilds. Jagex added it in 0.12.1, built *"to make you faster, leaner and meaner"* ([0.12.1 notes, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Update:0.12.1_is_now_live!)), and every 1.0.1+ raid skip uses at least one of its unlocks.

**Train Agility to 44** for the full route and **to 70** for a safety net while you practise.

## Which unlocks does the route use?

| Level | Unlock | Cost | Cooldown | Raid use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **5** | **Windstep** | 5 Air runes | 3 s | Air vent, Thane door, flight start |
| **10** | **Double Jump** | 10 stamina | none | Infinite double jump, zoops |
| **22** | **Hopper** | passive | none | Cheaper jumps |
| **32** | **Air Dash** | stamina | not listed | Soulscourge and necro skips |
| **35** | **Breakfall** | passive | none | Softer landings |
| **44** | **Phase Dash** | 20 Air + 10 Astral | 45 s | Demon skip, weak-zoop recovery |
| **57** | **Recall** | 30 Air + 15 Law | 75 s | Practice safety net |
| **70** | **Reactive Recall** | upgrade to Recall | uses the Recall tether | Fatal falls teleport you back |

Values come from the wiki's Agility table and spell pages ([Agility](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Agility)).

## What does Windstep need?

**Windstep** is the spell that makes you **jump up to 16 metres** and **fall more slowly**, with a **7-second window** to use it after casting ([Windstep, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Windstep)). It is the **first input** of ***Dotxy's*** flight loop and of ***Sir_Rebral's*** Thane-door windjump.

**The sources give two levels.** The Agility unlock table lists **Agility 5**; the Windstep infobox shows **level 8**, carried over from its Runecrafting days. Jagex moved it in 0.12.1: *"Windstep is now an Agility skill spell, rather than a Runecrafting skill spell."* ([0.12.1 notes, Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1374490/view/1839676055882020)). **Reach Agility 8** and both readings are covered.

**Leggings of Lightness** make Windstep cost **zero runes** ([gear post](/raid-speedrun-gear-and-consumables/)).

## What do Double Jump and Hopper add?

**Double Jump** is a **second jump in mid-air**, up to **4 metres**, with **no cooldown** and the same **10 stamina** as a normal jump ([Double Jump, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Double_Jump)). ***Dotxy's*** infinite double jump chains it after every Jump Attack cancel. **Hopper** at 22 makes each jump **cheaper**.

## What does Air Dash add?

**Air Dash** is the **10-metre horizontal dash** you get by pressing **evade while airborne** ([Air Dash, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Air_Dash)). ***Nanobit*** counts them in his run notes: *"Can do 4 air dash instead of 3 towards 1st air anima pillar"* ([7:01, 0:51](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=51)).

## What does Phase Dash add?

**Phase Dash** grants **extra Air Dashes while airborne, up to 3 per cast**, for **20 Air + 10 Astral** runes on a **45-second cooldown** ([Phase Dash, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Phase_Dash)). ***SamFPlays*** pairs it with Surge to clear the **Abyssal Demon** ([Phase Dash & Surge combo, 0:00](https://youtu.be/ESrrkdxD1fo?t=0)). ***Nanobit*** spends it like a budget: *"then you can save the phase dash for Kuldra"* ([7:01, 5:18](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=318)).

## What do Recall and Reactive Recall add?

**Recall** places a **runic marker**; recast to **teleport back**. At **Agility 70**, **Reactive Recall** fires **automatically on a fatal fall** and consumes the tether ([Reactive Recall, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Reactive_Recall)). Use it in **practice** to retry a vertical skip at once. The 0.12.1 notes keep Breakfall honest: *"Fatal falls will still kill you though."*

## How do you train Agility?

1. **Sprint everywhere.** The wiki lists **foot travel**, **courses**, **dodging vault traps** and **dodging enemy attacks** as XP sources ([Agility](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Agility)).
2. **Run the agility courses** in each region as you pass them.
3. **Follow the phase order**: Windstep and Double Jump in **Phase 0**, Air Dash in **Phase 2**, Phase Dash in **Phase 3**, Recall in **Phase 4**, Reactive Recall in **Phase 5** of the Raid Lab Hardcore route.

**Travel on foot pays more as you level.** The wiki: *"Players will gain 50/200/400 Agility XP at level 1/48/85, passively simply by running on foot across the world."* ([Agility, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Agility)). The **Brynmoor courses** open at **Agility 1**, so the first course is available from your first hour.

**Fund the runes as you train.** Windstep and Phase Dash use **Air runes** (Runecrafting 1) and Phase Dash adds **Astral runes** (Runecrafting 3). Recall adds **Law runes**: the wiki states *"Law runes can be crafted at a rune altar at level 50 Runecrafting or higher"* ([Law Rune, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Law_Rune)), while the Hardcore route lists Runecrafting 16. **Check the Law altar level in-game**, and collect Law runes from **Garou Packs** (5 to 10 at a 10/100 rate) in the meantime ([Garou Pack, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Garou_Pack)).

## What this means when you run

- **Hit Agility 44** so Phase Dash is ready for the Abyssal Demon and the Kuldra approach.
- **Count your dashes** per gap the way ***Nanobit*** does, and save Phase Dash for the end.
- **Practise with Reactive Recall** at Agility 70 so missed vertical skips cost seconds.
