The **Scorned Wilderness world record** is ***TexasPete's*** **6:50.000**, and it is won in the **first three splits**: he reaches the Keep **23.7 seconds** ahead of ***Nanobit's*** 7:01, then gives back **11.73 seconds** in Cataclysm Keep and Kuldra's Sanctum. This pillar measures every second of that, and turns it into **a pace you can run with**.

**Every number here is measured frame by frame**, on one shared trigger, so the runs compare directly.

## How were the splits measured?

**One trigger, every run.** Splits 1 to 4 sit on the first frame the **"EXTRACTION POINT UNLOCKED"** banner shows. Split 5 is the **cut to black after "GET OUT NOW!"**, which is speedrun.com's end rule.

| Step | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Banner detection** | Template matching at 10 fps, then checked frame by frame |
| **Timer offset** | The video second where the run timer reads 0, read from the on-screen timer at 3+ frames (agreement ±0.02 s) |
| **Precision** | ±0.1 s per split mark |
| **Corrections** | Any mark can be adjusted in the Raid Lab (Race the WR, Adjust marks) and exported |

## Is there a faster run the board has yet to see?

**There is one unverified lead.** ***IINo1goodII*** posted *"Kuldra skipped in 4 mins 40 seconds"* on 28 October and writes *"Sub 4 mins is possible. I'm confident someone could do it in around 3 minutes flat."* ([video](https://youtu.be/4hncfjhUvg8?t=0)). He keeps the method private: *"Not showing full clip or methods used.. sorry guys."*

His comment names three pieces: *"I launched myself skipping the 3rd checkpoint and some of the parkour area. Used the plunge mech to bypass the rocks on the fight itself since they are patched. Surge dashed the bridge."* The run sits outside speedrun.com and its timing method is unpublished. **Ask in the RSDW Speedrunning Discord** ([invite](https://discord.gg/aGZkuC5yK6)), the hub ***Nanobit*** points runners to.
