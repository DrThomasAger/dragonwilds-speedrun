**Fragments of Ghornfell** is the **second split** of the Scorned Wilderness raid, a Garou courtyard and a toxic castle that ends past the **Abyssal Demon** *(a teleporting demon mini-boss that follows you when you get far away)*. The 1.0.1.2 route clears it with **two skips**: the **poison area skip** and the **Surge and Phase Dash past the demon**.

***TexasPete*** runs it in **1:01.10** and ***Nanobit*** in **1:07.10**. ***TexasPete*** names this stretch in his commentary: *"Where all runs go to die."*

## What do you need?

| Requirement | Unlock | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Surge** | Magic 11, 10 Astral + 10 Law runes | Three long dodges past the demon |
| **Phase Dash** | Agility 44, 20 Air + 10 Astral runes | Extra air dashes after the Surge |
| **Jump Attack** | Attack 25 perk "Death From Above!" | Plunge cancels |
| **A targeted AoE spell** | On the spell ring (`Q`) | Powers the dive cancel |

See [Which spells and perks do raid skips use?](/spells-and-perks-for-raid-skips/) and [How do you combine Surge and Phase Dash?](/surge-and-phase-dash-combo/)

## How do you skip the poison area?

***SamFPlays*** routes the **entire poison area** as a skip. His follow-along shows the line and his guide explains it.

1. **Land from the Fragments zoop** and follow ***SamFPlays'*** line from the [explained chapter](https://youtu.be/ESrrkdxD1fo?t=260).
2. **Plunge cancel** *(cancel the jump-attack animation with a targeted AoE spell)* where ***Nanobit*** marks it, instead of waiting to fall.
3. **Push into the hole with confidence.** ***Nanobit***: more confidence here *"will produce better (faster) results"*.
4. **Zoop hard at 2:32.** A strong zoop carries you to his 2:37 spot **with zero dashes**.

## How do you get past the Abyssal Demon?

**The demon skip** is ***SamFPlays'*** **Surge then Phase Dash** in the air.

1. **Start the Surge from ***Nanobit's*** exact spot** ([7:01, 2:45](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=165)) so the demon stays in its area.
2. **Surge**, then **Phase Dash in the air** past the demon.
3. **Keep your line clear of the pillar** ahead, where a skeleton blocked ***Nanobit***.

***notmusty1*** posted a **full Abyssal Demon skip** for 1.0.1 on 17 October ([video](https://youtu.be/rNDeEKsKGeA?t=0)). Compare it with the Surge line and test it three times on 1.0.1.2 before you route it.

## How fast is each piece of Fragments?

**Fragments splits into three timed pieces** (about ±0.3 s): the fortress run to the courtyard, the poison area and demon, and the golden path to the portal.

| Segment | ***TexasPete*** 6:50 | ***Nanobit*** 7:01 | ***TexasPete*** 7:40 | ***SamFPlays*** 10:53 | Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fragments to fortress courtyard | 22.7 | 26.0 | **22.3** | 44.9 | 22.3 |
| Poison area and Abyssal Demon | **30.2** | **30.2** | 48.0 | 42.0 | 30.0 |
| Golden path to the portal | **8.2** | 10.9 | 8.3 | 10.4 | 8.2 |

**TexasPete's 6.0 s lead comes from the two ends of the split.** He is 3.3 s faster to the fortress courtyard and 2.7 s faster on the golden path; the poison area and demon tie at **30.2 s**.

## What this means when you run

- **Plunge cancel at the drop** instead of waiting to fall.
- **Start the Surge from Nanobit's 2:45 spot** so the demon stays behind you.
- **Aim the 2:32 zoop for distance** so you keep both dashes for later.
