**Sanctum flight** is ***Dotxy's*** **1.0.1.2** way through **Kuldra's Sanctum**: a loop of **jump attacks**, each one cancelled by a **targeted AoE spell** and followed by a fresh **jump**, that keeps you in the air as long as you repeat it. It replaces the **Windstep up the blocks** that 1.0.1.2 closed, and it carries you to the **fight skip** and the **rib clip**.

Kuldra's Sanctum is the **fifth split**. ***Nanobit*** runs it in **1:56.15** and ***TexasPete*** in **1:59.38**, and **both runs predate the flight**. This is the **biggest open save** in the record.

## What do you need?

| Requirement | Unlock | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Windstep** | Agility 5 (infobox shows 8) | Starting the flight |
| **Jump Attack** | Attack 25 perk "Death From Above!" | The plunge you cancel |
| **A targeted AoE spell** | On the spell ring (`Q`) | The cancel |
| **Surge** | Magic 11 | The bridge to Kuldra |
| **Phase Dash** | Agility 44 | Safety at the bridge |

See [Which spells and perks do raid skips use?](/spells-and-perks-for-raid-skips/) and [How do you do the infinite double jump?](/infinite-double-jump-sanctum-flight/)

## How do you fly through the Sanctum?

These are ***Dotxy's*** inputs, from his video description:

1. **Windstep.**
2. **Jump Attack.**
3. **`Q`** (opens the spell ring).
4. **Any targeted AoE spell.**
5. **`Esc`** (closes the spell ring).
6. **Jump.**
7. **Repeat steps 2 to 6** as long as you need height.

**Faster inputs gain height.** ***Dotxy***: *"If you're faster than I am you can gain height, not just hover"*. ***IINo1goodII*** adds that you can **dash and Phase Dash** inside the loop, and that he runs it **consistently on console**.

## How do you skip the Kuldra fight?

**The Kuldra fight skip** takes you past the boss to the **Heart**. ***Zilko*** found the original, credited by ***Tiger with the Bars***; ***CrossRSDW*** rebuilt it after 1.0.0.6; ***SamFPlays*** teaches the 1.0.1 version; ***FastTipsGaming*** showed it working after the latest patch on **27 October** and again in a full run on **2 November**.

1. **Fly up** where the route used to Windstep up the blocks.
2. **Follow ***SamFPlays'*** fight-skip line** ([guide 9:18](https://youtu.be/ESrrkdxD1fo?t=558)).
3. **Plunge past the rocks.** ***IINo1goodII*** describes using *"the plunge mech to bypass the rocks on the fight itself since they are patched"*.
4. **Surge across the bridge**, as ***IINo1goodII*** does.

## How do you do the rib clip?

**The rib clip** is a clip through the ground at **Kuldra's checkpoint**. ***Nanobit*** credits ***wes7918*** (***ChiefWes***) for it.

1. **Windjump into the exact spot** ***Nanobit*** shows at [5:33](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=333).
2. **Clip straight through** when your position is perfect: ***Nanobit*** measures **at least 3 s** saved over crouch-walking and a sword swing.
3. **Air dash twice** after it and keep your **Phase Dash** for the bridge.
4. **Stay to the RIGHT of the small white floating cube** on the bridge.
5. **Spend any Phase Dash you have left** at 6:20.

## Does it work on 1.0.1.2?

**The flight works on 1.0.1.2.** ***Nanobit*** confirms the old route closed and the new one opened: the blocks *"no longer works as of 1.0.1.2/1.0.1.3. You can still get up to that area via just flying"*. The **fight skip** is confirmed after the patch by ***FastTipsGaming***. Test the rib clip three times on 1.0.1.2 before you route it.

## How fast is the Sanctum flight?

**The Sanctum segment runs from the Sanctum door load to the Heart tunnel load** *(the two black loading frames either side of Kuldra's arena)*, so it holds the flight and the fight skip together.

| Route | Run | Time (s) | Status on 1.0.1.2 | Clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Windstep up the blocks | ***TexasPete*** 6:50 | 35.0 | Patched | [6:50, 5:20](https://youtu.be/6WEIHo7AUf0?t=320) |
| Windstep up the blocks | ***Nanobit*** 7:01 | 35.0 | Patched | [7:01, 5:50](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=350) |
| Windstep up the blocks | ***SamFPlays*** 10:53 | 49.4 | Patched | [follow-along, 8:18](https://youtu.be/WpvNjqVmpVM?t=498) |
| Infinite flight demo | ***Dotxy*** | **51.9** | Works (demo, no timer) | [0:00](https://youtu.be/GeWbo4GpFEg?t=0) |
| Drop into the void below the start platform | ***FastTipsGaming*** | 57.6 | Works (tutorial pace) | [8:04](https://youtu.be/oK0_U_I40DY?t=484) |

***Dotxy's*** demo shows **50.6 s** from the first Sanctum frame to the tunnel load, plus about **1.3 s** of door load. The demo **lands and walks the walkway** from 0:21 to 0:38, so a direct flight line beats it.

**The target is 49.0 s**, about 3 s under the demo. **This is the swing segment of the record.** The flight costs about **17 s** against the patched blocks, and a runner on target pace gives back about 6 s of an 8 s lead here. Taking the flight from about 52 s to 49 s is what makes sub-6:50 realistic.

**This is a test step.** Fly the whole Sanctum without landing, time it from door load to tunnel load, and route it once you reach 49 s three times.

## What this means when you run

- **Drill the six-input flight loop** until you gain height every cycle.
- **Hold a Phase Dash** through the rib clip for the bridge.
- **Stay right of the white cube** on the bridge to Kuldra.
