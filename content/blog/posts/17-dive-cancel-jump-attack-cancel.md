The **dive cancel** *(also called the jump-attack cancel, JAC or plunge cancel)* is ***Dotxy's*** technique for **ending the Jump Attack animation instantly**: you cast a targeted AoE spell during the plunge and cancel the cast. You land with **full control of your character** and zero ending lag.

***Dotxy*** posted it on **14 October 2026**, the day patch 1.0.1 added the Jump Attack. ***Nanobit*** used it in the 7:01 the same week: *"Thanks again to @Dotxygaming for the plunge-cancel tech, it's very fun."* ([7:01 description](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=0))

## What do you need for a dive cancel?

| Requirement | Unlock | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Death From Above!** | Attack 25 perk | Unlocks the Jump Attack |
| **Height** | Windstep or double jump | The attack triggers *"above the max height of the double jump's arc from the ground"*; *"Windstep counts here"* |
| **Targeted AoE spell** | on the spell ring (`Q`) | The cast that overrides the plunge |
| **Plunge weapon** | your mace | A weapon with a plunge animation |

## How do you do the dive cancel?

1. **Get airborne** with **Windstep** or a **double jump**.
2. **Jump Attack.** The plunge animation starts.
3. **Open the spell ring** (`Q`) and **select any targeted AoE spell** during the animation.
4. **Cancel the cast.** You have *"full movement over your character instantly"*.
5. **Move, jump or dash** straight away.

Watch ***Dotxy's*** [51-second demo](https://youtu.be/LGCddmk_TnM?t=0) **now** and copy the rhythm on flat ground first.

## Why does it work?

**Since 1.0, casting and movement overlap by design.** Jagex: *"This means you can opt out of those pesky wind-ups or spells you've committed to mid-combat."* ([1.0 patch notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1374490/view/1843481262704158)) The dive cancel uses that window: **the spell cast overrides the plunge**, and the cancel overrides the spell.

**It works off both jumps.** ***Dotxy***: *"this works with both windstep and double jumping, any time you're in the jump attack animation you can cancel it"*.

## Where does the route use it?

- **Fragments**: ***Nanobit*** at [2:13](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=133): *"Can likely plunge cancel here … instead of waiting to fall, saving a second maybe."*
- **Keep exit rocks**: [5:07](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=307) and [5:12](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=312), *"as well as using the cancel to dash onto the first rock that I jump from at 5:15."*
- **Kuldra's Sanctum**: chained dive cancels become **flight**. See [What is the infinite double jump in Dragonwilds?](/infinite-double-jump-sanctum-flight/)

## Which weapon should you hold?

**Hold a weapon with a plunge animation.** ***Nanobit***: *"remember that DScim doesn't have a plunge animation, so you need something else equipped."* ([7:01 comment, 5:07](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=307))

**Patch 1.0.1.2 touched the Dragon Scimitar:** *"Fixed an issue where the Dragon Scimitar would not allow users to Jump Attack in certain circumstances."* ([1.0.1.2 patch notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1374490/view/1847286114035869)) **Test the Dragon Scimitar on 1.0.1.2** before routing it; the mace from your gear list is the reliable choice.

## What this means when you run

- **Bind a targeted AoE spell** to the spell ring before your first practice session.
- **Equip the mace** for every section where you plunge cancel.
- **Add the cancels at 2:13, 5:07 and 5:12** once the dive cancel lands every time on flat ground.

- The dive cancel ends a Jump Attack instantly by casting and then cancelling a targeted AoE spell.
