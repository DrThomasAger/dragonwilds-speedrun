A **zoop** is the **long launch off an edge** that carries you from one Scorned Wilderness island to the next, made by inputting the dash on the frame your character walks off the lip. It is the **most precise input** in the run and the **largest single source** of time between a 7:01 and a sub-7.

***Nanobit*** puts it at the centre of his run notes: the 7:01 *"needs very clean movement and strong zoops. Especially in the area with the 3rd BD directly after zooping from keep"* ([7:01 description](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=0)).

## What do you need to zoop?

| Requirement | Unlock | Role in the zoop |
|---|---|---|
| **Air Dash** | Agility 32 | The dash you input at the edge |
| **Phase Dash** | Agility 44 | Recovery when a zoop comes up short |
| **Surge** | Magic 11 | Extra recovery distance |
| **Fleet-footed Infusion** | consumable | Sprint stamina for the run-up |

## How do you zoop?

***Nanobit*** describes the timing for the hardest zoop in the run, **Vines to Fragments**:

1. **Walk or run** to the end of the flat part of the launch rock.
2. **Watch the lip.** The rock has *"an incredibly small slope after the flat part, before it drops off completely."*
3. **Input the dash** *"either on the frame you start walking off the rock … or 1 before."*
4. **Read the result.** *"Any earlier and you smash your ankles into the side Fragments and fall to your death, any later and it becomes an air dash."*
5. **Walk the approach** while you learn it. ***Nanobit*** found *"walking allows more time to react"*, with the same distance as running.

Load his [Vines to Fragments zoop at 1:45](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=105) **now** and step through the lip frame by frame.

## Where does the route zoop?

These are the zoops ***Nanobit*** marks as time saves in his 7:01, at video time:

| Video time | Where | Nanobit's note |
|---|---|---|
| [1:45](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=105) | Vines to Fragments | "Technically could start zoop to Fragments about 1.5s earlier but this zoop is hard, so safety." |
| [2:05](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=125) | Keep wall across the pond | "can make it all the way to the gate with a good one, save maybe 2.5s" |
| [2:32](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=152) | Fragments | "you can make it all the way to where I'm standing at 2:37, with no air/phase dash at all." |
| [3:46](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=226) | Stronghold | "This zoop can go far enough to require no dashes." |
| [3:50](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=230) | Stronghold to Keep | "This saves A LOT of time if it works." |
| [5:18](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=318) | Keep exit | "Sometimes this zoop is strong enough to only need 1 dash, then you can save the phase dash for Kuldra." |
| [5:57](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=357) | Sanctum approach | "With a strong enough zoop you don't need an air or phase dash." |

**The pattern is consistent:** a strong zoop saves the dashes, and saved dashes buy time later at Kuldra.

## Is there an easier Fragments skip?

**Yes.** ***Nanobit*** points learners to his slower, safer version: *"if you want an easier (but slower) skip to Fragments I suggest checking out my Day 1 Strats Video … for the zoop from necro island into respawn skip"* ([7:01 comment](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=105)). The day-one steps read: *"zoop left side off rocks left of bridge towards top of pillar on left, needs decent height, may or may not need both dashes depending on strength of zoop"* ([8:32 description](https://youtu.be/vw_Mr5TGJAg?t=0)).

**The skip itself traces to *SectorTSWG* via *Sir_Rebral*:** *"The skip to Fragments of Ghornfell I saw in a video by @SectorTSWG"* ([Sir_Rebral, 0:53](https://youtu.be/r1nrG2NlW-g?t=53)).

## Does framerate change zoop distance?

**This is the open experiment of the route.** ***Nanobit***: *"I think lower framerates can give better zoops as well, just from random observations, but I can't/haven't really tested that."* ***Trance_OCE*** proposes the test: *"see if zooping and distance is tied to FPS (is the case on the tiger engine /destiny 2 and worldlining)"*.

**Run the test yourself:** cap your framerate at two values, take 20 zoops from the same rock at each, and mark the landing spot.

## What this means when you run

- **Walk the approach** to the Fragments zoop until the frame timing feels automatic.
- **Learn the necro-island respawn version first** and switch to the direct zoop once it lands every time.
- **Bank every dash a strong zoop saves** and spend it at Kuldra.

## Key takeaways

- A zoop is a dash input on the exact frame your character walks off the edge, or one frame before.
