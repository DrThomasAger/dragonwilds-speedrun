The **community sum of best** is the total of the **fastest measured segment** for each of the five splits, taken from any record run: for the Scorned Wilderness raid it is **398.27 seconds, 6:38.27**. The **target pace** that beats the record on today's patch is **6:48.10**, built from pieces that still work on 1.0.1.2.

*(A **sum of best** is the time a perfect run would score if every split matched the best version seen.)*

## What are the six measured runs?

Segment times in seconds, all on the same extraction-banner trigger (±0.1 s).

| Run | Vines | Fragments | Stronghold | Keep | Sanctum | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ***TexasPete*** 6:50 | **81.52** | **61.10** | **51.70** | 96.30 | 119.38 | 410.00 |
| ***Nanobit*** 7:01 | 87.82 | 67.10 | 63.10 | **87.80** | **116.15** | 421.97 |
| ***TexasPete*** 7:40 | 89.95 | 78.60 | 54.40 | 101.80 | 135.87 | 460.62 |
| ***Nimblesk*** 8:28 | 100.75 | 78.20 | 58.50 | 98.60 | 172.70 | 508.75 |
| ***ChiefWes*** 9:37 | 119.93 | 89.40 | 61.60 | 125.90 | 180.50 | 577.33 |
| ***SamFPlays*** 10:53 | 115.20 | 97.30 | 100.30 | 168.60 | 172.00 | 653.40 |

Bold marks the fastest segment in each column: 81.52 + 61.10 + 51.70 + 87.80 + 116.15 = **398.27 s**. ***TexasPete's*** 7:40 total is the measured cut to black; his on-screen timer stopped early, *"I accidently stopped the clock less than a second short. Total time was roughly 7.40.84"*.

## Why is the target slower than the sum of best?

**The 6:38.27 includes patched pieces.** Its Stronghold segment is ***TexasPete's*** Thane-less wall climb, and both of its Sanctum-era runs climbed the blocks to Kuldra, which 1.0.1.2 closed. ***Nanobit***: *"Windstepping up the blocks to Kuldra no longer works as of 1.0.1.2/1.0.1.3."*

**The run path rebuilds the route from valid pieces.** Cut into 13 checkpoint pieces and kept to what works on 1.0.1.2, the fastest composite is **6:52.80**. The target takes **4.7 seconds** of named saves off it to reach **6:48.10**. The full method is in [the skip-level path](/skip-level-optimal-path/).

| Number | Time | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Split-level sum of best** | 6:38.27 | Best of six runs per split, patched strats included |
| **Checkpoint sum of best, any patch** | 6:35.70 | Best of every one of 13 pieces, patched strats included |
| **1.0.1.2 composite** | 6:52.80 | Fastest pieces that work on 1.0.1.2, three unverified |
| **Target pace** | **6:48.10** | The composite minus 4.7 s of named saves |

## What are the split targets?

| Split | Target segment | Target at split | ***TexasPete*** 6:50 | ***Nanobit*** 7:01 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Dragontail Vines** | 82.7 | **1:22.70** | 1:21.52 | 1:27.82 |
| **Fragments of Ghornfell** | 60.5 | **2:23.20** | 2:22.62 | 2:34.92 |
| **The Shattered Stronghold** | 52.5 | **3:15.70** | 3:14.32 | 3:38.02 |
| **Cataclysm Keep** | 86.6 | **4:42.30** | 4:50.62 | 5:05.82 |
| **Kuldra's Sanctum** | 125.8 | **6:48.10** | 6:50.00 | 7:01.97 |

**Read the shape:** level with the 6:50 through the Stronghold, **8.3 s ahead** at the Keep exit thanks to the Keep hole, then a Sanctum target **6.42 s slower** than TexasPete's, because flight takes longer than the patched blocks.

## What do the runners' own sums of best say?

**They agree with the target.** ***Nanobit*** on the 7:04: *"Overall just doing things more cleanly will produce a sub 7 considering my sum of best is 6:58."* On the 7:01: *"clearly can be done sub 7 judging by my sum of best"*. The Raid Lab dataset records his 7:01 sum of best as **6:51 (411 s)**.

## What this means when you run

- **Load the 6:48.10 split targets** into your timer as the comparison for every attempt.
- **Bank the Keep lead**: hit 4:42.30 at the Keep exit.
- **Hold 125.8 s or better** across the Sanctum and heart, the segment flight decides.
