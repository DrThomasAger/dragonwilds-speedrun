**Nanobit's time saves** are the **27 timestamped notes** in his pinned comment on the 7:01, each one a place where his own run lost time: *"How to save time and some general tips"*. The Raid Lab run path puts an estimated cost on each note, and together they come to **about 29.0 seconds**, enough to take a 7:01 under the record.

**The timestamps are video time**, so every link opens on the exact moment. The **Est. s** column is the run path's estimate from frame timing; a 0.0 marks a note about consistency or reference points.

**Notes 23 to 26 carry 0.0** because they sit on the blocks route that 1.0.1.2 closed. ***Nanobit***: *"You can still get up to that area via just flying lmao."* Flight replaces that climb ([Sanctum flight](/infinite-double-jump-sanctum-flight/)).

## Which saves should you take first?

1. **The heart safespot (6.0 s)** and **the rib clip (3.0 s)**: the two largest, worth 9 seconds together.
2. **The Stronghold to Keep zoop (4.0 s)** and **the pond zoop (2.5 s)**: they need [zoop practice](/what-is-a-zoop/).
3. **Free saves**: notes 1, 13, 14 and 18 cost only attention, 4.0 s in all.
4. **Tech saves**: notes 9, 19 and 20 need the [dive cancel](/dive-cancel-jump-attack-cancel/).

## What this means when you run

- **Jump straight into the melee safespot** at the heart, the single largest save at 6 seconds.
- **Sprint once** at the start and re-drink the Fleet-footed Infusion at the Stronghold.
- **Add the fourth air dash** to the first air anima pillar this session.
