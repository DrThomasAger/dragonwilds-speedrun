**The fastest Scorned Wilderness speedrun route** is ***Nanobit's*** **7:01 route** *(the fastest run on the 1.0.1 ruleset)* with ***Dotxy's*** **Sanctum flight** in the final split. It carries you from the loading screen to the cut to black after Kuldra's Heart in **five splits**, and it **works on patch 1.0.1.2**.

This pillar teaches that route **one skip group at a time**, in the exact order you play it. Every skip names **who found it**, shows **where it happens** in a record run, and links the clip.

## Why build on the 7:01 instead of the 6:50?

***TexasPete's*** **6:50** is the record to beat and the **best movement reference** in the game. Its route belongs to an earlier patch: ***Nanobit*** says it is *"using some strats that have been patched at this point"*.

***Nanobit's*** **7:01** runs the **1.0.1 route** that survives today, with one change he writes into his own description: *"Windstepping up the blocks to Kuldra no longer works as of 1.0.1.2/1.0.1.3. You can still get up to that area via just flying lmao."* That flight is ***Dotxy's*** tech, taught in the [Sanctum post](/kuldras-sanctum-flight-and-fight-skip/).

## What are the five splits?

**A split** is one timed section of the run. The community split file ends splits 1 to 4 on the **"EXTRACTION POINT UNLOCKED"** banner *(the message when you reach a Zilyana Teleporter)* and split 5 on the **cut to black** after "GET OUT NOW!", which is the speedrun.com end rule.

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Split** | One timed section of the run, ended by a fixed on-screen trigger. |
| **Zoop** | A long launch off an edge that carries you between islands. |
| **Skip** | A route piece that bypasses the intended path, puzzle or fight. |
| **Sum of best** | The total of the fastest segment for every split. |

Here is every split, measured **frame by frame** (±0.1 s) in the [Raid Lab](https://drthomasager.github.io/dragonwilds-speedrun/):

| Split | ***TexasPete*** 6:50 | ***Nanobit*** 7:01 | Faster |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Dragontail Vines | **1:21.52** | 1:27.82 | TexasPete by 6.3 s |
| 2. Fragments of Ghornfell | **1:01.10** | 1:07.10 | TexasPete by 6.0 s |
| 3. The Shattered Stronghold | **0:51.70** | 1:03.10 | TexasPete by 11.4 s |
| 4. Cataclysm Keep | 1:36.30 | **1:27.80** | Nanobit by 8.5 s |
| 5. Kuldra's Sanctum | 1:59.38 | **1:56.15** | Nanobit by 3.2 s |
| **Total** | **6:50.00** | **7:01.97** | |

***TexasPete*** wins the **first three splits**. ***Nanobit*** wins the **Keep** and the **Sanctum**. The fastest segment from each adds up to **6:38.27**.

## What is the route in play order?

1. **[Dragontail Vines](/dragontail-vines-split-route/)**: the air anima Windstep, the soulscourge skip and the black dragon skip.
2. **[The skip to Fragments of Ghornfell](/skip-to-fragments-of-ghornfell/)**: the zoop past the necromancer area, found by ***SectorTSWG*** via ***Sir_Rebral***.
3. **[Fragments of Ghornfell](/fragments-of-ghornfell-poison-and-abyssal-demon-skip/)**: the poison area skip and the Abyssal Demon skip.
4. **[The Shattered Stronghold](/shattered-stronghold-garou-thane-skip/)**: ***Sir_Rebral's*** Garou Thane kill through the gate.
5. **[Cataclysm Keep](/cataclysm-keep-hole-elemental-puzzle-skip/)**: the hole found by ***IINo1goodII*** and ***notmusty1***.
6. **[Kuldra's Sanctum](/kuldras-sanctum-flight-and-fight-skip/)**: ***Dotxy's*** flight, the fight skip and the rib clip.
7. **[Kuldra's Heart](/kuldras-heart-where-to-stand/)**: where to stand, and the cut to black.

## Where is the time left?

***Nanobit*** names the place himself: *"Especially in the area with the 3rd BD directly after zooping from keep, if you get a strong enough one you can make it all the way from where you landed to well past the plant."*

**The biggest open save is the Sanctum.** Both record Sanctum segments **predate** ***Dotxy's*** **1.0.1.2 flight**, so a clean flight is the clearest path under 6:50.

## What is the optimal path on 1.0.1.2?

**The optimal path** is the fastest chain of route pieces that still work on 1.0.1.2, timed **checkpoint to checkpoint** across four runs (about ±0.3 s, read from frame strips). It adds up to **6:52.80**. The **target pace** that beats the record is **6:48.10**, and the extra time comes mostly from the **Sanctum flight**, the **Heart safespot** and the **Keep**.

**The pace shape is level, ahead, then hold.** Run level with the 6:50 through split 3, reach split 4 about **8 s ahead** thanks to the 1.0.1 Keep hole, give back about **6 s** in the Sanctum (the flight is slower than the patched blocks), and take about **4 s** back at the Heart. Taking the flight from about **52 s to 49 s** is what turns sub-6:50 into a routine target.

## What this means when you run

- **Learn the full route slowly first** with ***SamFPlays'*** [11-minute follow-along](https://youtu.be/WpvNjqVmpVM?t=0), then add one skip group per session.
- **Copy TexasPete's movement** in splits 1 to 3 and **Nanobit's route** in splits 4 and 5.
- **Check each skip's status** in [Patch Watch](/what-patch-1-0-1-2-changed-for-raid-speedruns/) before every practice session.
