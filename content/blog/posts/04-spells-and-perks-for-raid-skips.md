**Raid spells and perks** are the **Magic, Attack and Artisan unlocks** the route casts on top of Agility: **Surge** for long dodges, the ***Death From Above!*** **Jump Attack** with **Magical Mending** as the cancel spell for ***Dotxy's*** cancels, **Tempest Shield** as a panic button and **Enfeeble** for the Heart.

**Three of them are mandatory**: Surge, the Jump Attack and a targeted AoE cancel spell.

## Which spells and perks matter?

| Unlock | Level | Cost | Cooldown | Raid use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Surge** | Magic 11 | 10 Astral + 10 Law | 45 s | Abyssal Demon skip, Kuldra approach |
| **Jump Attack** (*Death From Above!*) | Attack 25 perk | 15 stamina | none listed | Dive cancel, infinite double jump, flight |
| **Magical Mending** (cancel spell) | Artisan 56 | 20 Earth + 10 Nature | 60 s | The cast you cancel in the dive cancel |
| **Superheat** (lower option) | Artisan 20 | 5 Air + 15 Fire | 10 s | Alternative cancel spell |
| **Tempest Shield** | Attack 11 | 15 Air | 45 s | Blocks all damage while held |
| **Enchant Weapon: Air** (optional) | Attack 30 | 15 Air + 5 Astral | 90 s | Faster Garou Thane kill |
| **Enfeeble** | Magic 61 | 20 Water + 20 Astral | 75 s | Heart phase |

Values come from the wiki spell pages, cited below.

## Which runes do you need?

| Rune | Runecrafting level (wiki) | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| **Air** | 1 | Windstep, Phase Dash, Tempest Shield, Superheat |
| **Astral** | 3 | Surge, Phase Dash, Enfeeble |
| **Earth** | 15 | Magical Mending |
| **Fire** | 25 | Superheat |
| **Nature** | 38 | Magical Mending |
| **Law** | 50 | Surge, Recall |

**Law runes carry a source conflict.** The wiki states *"Law runes can be crafted at a rune altar at level 50 Runecrafting or higher"* ([Law Rune, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Law_Rune)); the Raid Lab Hardcore route lists Runecrafting 16. **Check the altar in-game.** **Garou Packs** drop **5 to 10 Law runes** at a **10/100** rate ([Garou Pack, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Garou_Pack)), which keeps Surge stocked while you level.

**Rune totals per run are a count you make yourself.** Count your casts in one clean practice run and stock double that.

## What does Surge do?

**Surge** is a Magic spell that gives **three long dodges of 8 metres** each, with the charges lasting **two minutes** ([Surge, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Surge)). ***SamFPlays*** built the **Phase Dash and Surge combo** on it: *"click that like whatever your dash button is to activate surge. At the end when your momentum starts to die, jump and then double dash."* ([Every Skip Explained, 0:58](https://youtu.be/ESrrkdxD1fo?t=58)).

Since 1.0 you **cast Surge while moving**: *"you can now move while casting a range of spells, including Enchant Weapon, Windstep and Surge"* ([1.0 notes, Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1374490/view/1843481262704158)).

## What is the Jump Attack?

**The Jump Attack** is the **plunging melee attack** unlocked by the Attack perk ***Death From Above!*** at **Attack 25** ([Death From Above!, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Death_From_Above!)). Jagex describes the trigger in 1.0.1: *"above the max height of the double jump's arc from the ground, inputting an attack will plunge you downward to slam into unfortunate victims below... And yes, Windstep counts here."* ([1.0.1 notes, Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1374490/view/1846651706673783)).

**Hold a melee weapon with a plunge animation.** ***Nanobit*** warns: *"remember that DScim doesn't have a plunge animation, so you need something else equipped"* ([7:01, 5:07](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=307)). Jagex's 1.0.1.2 notes *"Fixed an issue where the Dragon Scimitar would not allow users to Jump Attack in certain circumstances"*, so **test the Dragon Scimitar's plunge in-game** before you carry it as your only melee weapon.

## Why is Magical Mending the cancel spell?

**The cancel spell** is the targeted AoE cast that ***Dotxy's*** **dive cancel** interrupts: *"instantly exit the jump attack animation and ending lag by casting a targeted AOE spell, cancel the cast to gain full movement over your character instantly"* ([Dive Cancel, description](https://youtu.be/LGCddmk_TnM?t=0)). Any targeted AoE spell works, and the community default is **Magical Mending**. ***FastTipsGaming***: *"Everyone pretty much agrees that magical mending is the best one to use because most people unlock it earlier than the others."* ([Infinite Jump, 0:17](https://youtu.be/U0_KPXu28qw?t=17)).

**Superheat** at **Artisan 20** is the lower option, and it sits on ***Dotxy's*** own ring in his flight video ([Sanctum flight, 0:07](https://youtu.be/GeWbo4GpFEg?t=7)). Whether a cancelled cast spends runes or starts the cooldown is a **test step**: cast-and-cancel ten times and watch your rune count.

## Which ring slots do the runners use?

| Spell | Ring slot | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Cancel spell** | Slot 1 (`Q` `1`) | ***Nanobit*** |
| **Surge** | Slot 3 (`Q` `3`) | ***loopuleasa*** |
| **Phase Dash** | Slot 4 (`Q` `4`) | ***loopuleasa*** |
| **Windstep** | Slot 7 (`Q` `7`) | ***Nanobit*** |

***Nanobit***: *"I'm using Q1E for the plunge-cancel tech, and Q7 a lot of the time to windstep."* ([reply on the 7:01](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=0)). Full keybinds are in the [setup checklist](/raid-speedrun-setup-checklist/).

## What do Tempest Shield and Enfeeble add?

**Tempest Shield** *"prevents all damage while blocking, including normally unblockable attacks"* for **15 Air runes** at **Attack 11** ([Tempest Shield, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Tempest_Shield)). It is the **safety option** on the hardcore-safe build.

**Enfeeble** at **Magic 61** weakens the target's attacks; the wiki page is a stub with no effect size ([Enfeeble, wiki](https://dragonwilds.runescape.wiki/w/Enfeeble)). The Raid Lab requirement list attaches it to the **Heart phase**.

## When do you unlock each one?

On the Raid Lab Hardcore route, **Tempest Shield**, **Surge** and **Superheat** arrive in **Phase 2** (Ghornfell, the level-11 power spike). **Magical Mending** arrives in **Phase 3** (Fellhollow). *Death From Above!* lands between Attack 11 and Attack 30, in **Phase 4**. **Enfeeble** arrives in **Phase 5** (Umbral Sands).

## What this means when you run

- **Unlock Death From Above!** at Attack 25 and carry a melee weapon with a plunge animation.
- **Put Magical Mending on slot 1** and Windstep on slot 7, the way ***Nanobit*** does.
- **Stock Astral and Law runes** for Surge, from the altar or from Garou Packs.
