***The speedrun.com Raid rules*** are the timing and video rules that decide **what counts as a Scorned Wilderness record**. The timer starts on **the first frame the loading icon disappears** going into the raid and stops on **the frame the screen goes black** after the "Get out now!" countdown.

Knowing the exact frames matters: **the escape after Kuldra's Heart is part of your time**, and a run without audible game audio stays off the board.

## When does the timer start and stop?

The Raid category rules read, in full:

> "Timer starts the first frame the loading icon disappears going into the raid. Timer ends on the frame the screen goes black after the "Get out now!" timer ends after defeating Kuldra's Heart." ([speedrun.com](https://www.speedrun.com/Runescape_Dragonwilds))

| Moment | Rule | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Start** | First frame the loading icon disappears going into the raid | Start LiveSplit on that frame, or let a moderator retime from the video. |
| **Splits 1 to 4** | Community split file: the "Extraction point unlocked" banner | Split on the first banner frame of each area. |
| **End** | Frame the screen goes black after the "Get out now!" timer | Keep running through the escape; the cut to black is your final split. |
| **Timing method** | Real time, milliseconds shown | Every frame of the escape counts. |

***TexasPete's*** **7:40** shows why the end frame matters: *"I accidently stopped the clock less than a second short. Total time was roughly 7.40.84"* ([7:40 description](https://youtu.be/6N3NwYA5Zh0?t=0)). The measured cut to black in that video sits at **460.62 s**.

## What must the video show?

The game-wide rules apply to every Raid run:

> "All submissions requires a full video of the run, including game audio. Game audio must be audible. The video link MUST embed on the run page (e.g. no URL shorteners or sketchy file sharing sites)" ([speedrun.com](https://www.speedrun.com/Runescape_Dragonwilds))

Two more lines from the same rules shape your recording setup:

- **Persistent VODs**: "VODs should persist indefinitely (e.g. Highlight instead of Past Broadcast)".
- **Visible HUD**: "Dynamic HUD elements should not be obscured or omitted (e.g. health, inventory, stamina)". Place your LiveSplit and webcam overlays **clear of the health, inventory and stamina bars**.

Every run is checked by the moderators, ***Archy*** and ***dotyoureyes***.

## Which bracket does a 1.0.1.2 run go in?

The Raid board has **two version brackets**: **Pre 1.0.0.6** and **1.0.0.6+**. A run on **1.0.1.2** goes in **1.0.0.6+**, the same bracket as the 6:50 and the 7:01.

The Pre 1.0.0.6 bracket holds ***ShadowStalkerTM's*** **3:27** and ***heartlessedly's*** **3:57**, played on the extraction skip that 1.0.0.6 closed.

## Are macros allowed for the flight loop?

***Dotxy*** suggests one for the flight: *"Feel free to set up a macro for easy infinite flight!"* ([Sanctum flight](https://youtu.be/GeWbo4GpFEg?t=0)). The written Raid rules name **timing and video only**, and the game rules add a **good-faith clause**:

> "Runs are expected to be performed in good faith and may be rejected based on mod discretion if performed otherwise." ([speedrun.com](https://www.speedrun.com/Runescape_Dragonwilds))

So **ask the moderators before you submit a macro run**, and practise the loop by hand in the meantime. Hand inputs are accepted under every reading of the rules. Learn them in **[What is the infinite double jump?](/infinite-double-jump-sanctum-flight/)**.

## What this means when you run

- Split on the **first frame of the "Extraction point unlocked" banner** and run straight through the escape to the cut to black.
- Record **full video with audible game audio**, export a permanent highlight, and keep the HUD clear.
- Learn the flight loop **by hand** until the moderators rule on macros.
