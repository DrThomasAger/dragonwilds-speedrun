**Movement tech** is the set of **movement primitives** *(short, repeatable input sequences that move you further or faster than the intended path)* that every Scorned Wilderness skip is built from. Five primitives cover the whole 1.0.1.2 route: the **zoop**, the **dive cancel**, the **infinite double jump**, **Surge plus Phase Dash** and the **windjump**.

**The record lives in this pillar.** ***Nanobit*** names the requirement for a sub-7 directly: the run *"needs very clean movement and strong zoops"* ([7:01 description](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=0)).

## Why does movement decide the record?

**Patch 1.0.1 changed the movement kit.** Jagex added the **Jump Attack** and confirmed it works off Windstep: *"And yes, Windstep counts here. What goes up, must come crashing down."* ([1.0.1 patch notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1374490/view/1846651706673783))

**The same day, *Dotxy* turned it into a cancel**, and within nine days the cancel became **flight**. ***Nanobit*** sums up the arc: *"Plunge-cancel tech has evolved even further"* ([7:01 description](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=0)).

**The 6:50 predates all of it.** ***TexasPete*** recorded it *"prior to the windstep double jump and some other new routing strats"* ([6:50 description](https://youtu.be/6WEIHo7AUf0?t=0)). The new primitives are **where the time below 6:50 comes from**.

## What are the five primitives?

## In what order should you learn them?

1. **Windjump first.** It needs only **Windstep** (Agility 5) and it appears in every split.
2. **Surge plus Phase Dash second.** It powers the **Abyssal Demon** skip and the **Sanctum approach**.
3. **Dive cancel third.** It is the **building block** of flight.
4. **Infinite double jump fourth.** It turns the dive cancel into **Sanctum flight**, the swing segment of a sub-6:50.
5. **Zoops last.** They are the **most precise** inputs in the run and they repay hundreds of attempts.

## What this means when you run

- **Practise the windjump** on every ledge you meet until it lands first time.
- **Learn the dive cancel on flat ground** before you take it into the Sanctum.
- **Save zoops for last** and give them their own practice block every session.
