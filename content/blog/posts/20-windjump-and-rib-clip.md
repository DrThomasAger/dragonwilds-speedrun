A **windjump** is a **jump made under Windstep** *(the Agility spell that raises jump height to 16 metres and slows your fall)*, used to reach a ledge, a rock or a doorway in one move. The **rib clip** is the windjump at **Kuldra's checkpoint** that drops you **through the ground** at her ribs, found by ***wes7918*** (***ChiefWes***).

**Every split uses a windjump.** The rib clip is the single largest movement save in Nanobit's Sanctum notes: *"This saves at least 3s."*

## What do you need?

| Term | What it means | Unlock |
|---|---|---|
| **Windstep** | Higher jump, slow fall, 7-second window after the cast, 3 s cooldown | Agility 5, 5 Air runes |
| **Windjump** | A jump taken inside the Windstep window | Windstep |
| **Leggings of Lightness** | Windstep costs zero runes while worn | Temple Woods windmill vestige |
| **Rib clip** | A windjump at Kuldra's ribs that puts you through the ground | Windstep, a melee swing as backup |

## How do you windjump the Stronghold air door?

***Nanobit's*** day-one notes, with the Thane route credited to ***Sir_Rebral***:

1. **Kill the air-anima Garou Thane** through the closed gate with a melee weapon.
2. **Take the air charge** from the kill.
3. **Windjump up to the air door** *"from tip of rubble pile and go through"*.
4. **Exit** *"through hole in wall above orb dispenser"*.

Source: [8:32 description](https://youtu.be/vw_Mr5TGJAg?t=0). See it in SamFPlays' run at [3:40](https://youtu.be/WpvNjqVmpVM?t=220).

## How do you do the rib clip?

1. **Windjump** from the rock before Kuldra's checkpoint.
2. **Hit into the ribs** at the exact position. ***Nanobit***: *"If you're incredibly lucky, or smart enough to figure out the exact position to jump from and hit into, you can clip straight through the ground at rib clip"*.
3. **Fallback:** crouch walk up the rib and swing to clip out, the slower version Nanobit replaces.
4. **Continue** to the Sanctum approach.

Watch [5:33 in the 7:01](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=333) **now**, and the swing at [5:38](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=338) that ***loopuleasa*** called *"CHEFS KISS"*.

## Who found the rib clip?

**Credit goes to *wes7918*.** ***Nanobit*** lists the source in his 7:04 notes: *"@wes7918 (Rib Clip at Kuldra Checkpoint)"* ([7:04 description](https://youtu.be/KljLK0Z-9jY?t=0)). ***wes7918*** describes it himself: *"i was the one who found the strat to jump on the bone and punch up to get back out into the map"* ([7:01 comment](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=0)).

## Which windjumps changed with the patches?

**Patch 1.0.1 targeted Windstep:** *"Fixed several Windstep-related skips in the Scorned Wilderness Raid."* ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1374490/view/1846651706673783)) **1.0.1.2** removed TexasPete's rock landing; ***Keimbho***: *"the new update made the rock a phantom. Can't land on it anymore."* ([6:50 comment](https://youtu.be/6WEIHo7AUf0?t=0)) **Test the rib clip on 1.0.1.2** three times before you route it.

## What this means when you run

- **Windjump from the rubble tip** to the Stronghold air door every attempt.
- **Practise the rib-clip position** until the clip lands without the crouch walk.
- **Retest every windjump** after each patch before you route it.
