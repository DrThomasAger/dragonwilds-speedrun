The **Surge and Phase Dash combo** is ***SamFPlays'*** chain of **Surge dodges** *(three 8-metre dodges from one cast)* and **Phase Dash** *(extra air dashes while airborne)* that covers long horizontal gaps with almost no stamina. It carries you **past the Abyssal Demon** and across the **Sanctum approach**.

***SamFPlays*** opens his every-skip guide with it, in a chapter titled *"Phase Dash & Surge Combo"* ([guide, 0:00](https://youtu.be/ESrrkdxD1fo?t=0)).

## What do you need for the combo?

| Spell / skill | Unlock | Cost | Cooldown | What it gives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Surge** | Magic 11 | 10 Astral + 10 Law runes | 45 s | 3 dodges of 8 m, charges last 2 minutes, no stamina per dodge |
| **Air Dash** | Agility 32 | stamina | none listed | 10 m dash while airborne |
| **Phase Dash** | Agility 44 | 20 Air + 10 Astral runes | 45 s | Extra air dashes, up to 3 per cast |

Values are from the Dragonwilds wiki pages cited in the Raid Lab mechanics wiki. Rune and level routes live in [Get Raid-Ready](/what-do-you-need-to-speedrun-the-scorned-wilderness-raid/).

## How do you do the combo?

1. **Cast Surge first.** Its three dodges each travel 8 metres, and they cost no stamina.
2. **Jump** off the edge.
3. **Chain Phase Dash in the air** for the extra distance.
4. **Spend the Surge charges** inside their 2-minute window.
5. **Bind both to number keys.** ***loopuleasa***: *"I spam Q+3 and Q+4 to enable surge and dash"*.

Watch the [Abyssal Demon skip in SamFPlays' run at 3:04](https://youtu.be/WpvNjqVmpVM?t=184) **now**, then his [explained version at 5:00](https://youtu.be/ESrrkdxD1fo?t=300).

## Where does the route spend it?

- **Abyssal Demon (Fragments).** ***Nanobit*** at [2:45](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=165): *"it's important to start surging from exactly where I do, so the demon doesn't chase you into the next area."* ***notmusty1*** posted a full demon skip: *"skip the Abyssal Demon completely! Fully working as today 17.10.2026"* ([video](https://youtu.be/rNDeEKsKGeA?t=0)).
- **Keep exit.** [5:18](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=318): a strong zoop needs one dash, *"then you can save the phase dash for Kuldra."*
- **Sanctum approach.** [5:52](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=352): *"You can probably air dash at least twice here (don't waste phase dash yet though)"*; [6:20](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=380): *"If you have any phase dashes left, just use them now."*

## How should you budget the cooldowns?

**One cast per spell per segment.** Both spells sit on a **45-second cooldown**, so plan each cast like a resource.

***Nanobit*** weighs the Surge timing at Kuldra in his 7:04 notes: casting earlier and recasting at the top *"could still be faster, and save me a surge from the second side of Kuldra's bridge to her face (instead of jump/dashing), as well as one for the throat"* ([7:04 description](https://youtu.be/KljLK0Z-9jY?t=0)). **Test both timings** and keep the faster one.

## What this means when you run

- **Start the demon Surge** from Nanobit's exact spot at 2:45 every attempt.
- **Hold your Phase Dash** through the Keep exit and spend it at Kuldra.
- **Bind Surge and Phase Dash** to number keys before your next session.
