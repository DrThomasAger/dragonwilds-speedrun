***Patch 1.0.1.2*** is the **23 October 2026** Dragonwilds hotfix whose raid line reads, in full, **"Additional fixes for Kuldra Skips."** It is the **current patch** for raid speedruns, and the patch every route post in this series is checked against.

Jagex lists **one raid line**, and the community mapped the rest by testing. This post collects **what they found**, with the source for each finding.

## What closed in 1.0.1.2?

**1. The Windstep climb up the blocks to Kuldra.** ***Nanobit*** states it at the top of his 7:01 description: *"Windstepping up the blocks to Kuldra no longer works as of 1.0.1.2/1.0.1.3."* ([7:01 description](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=0))

**2. TexasPete's rock near Kuldra.** Commenters on the 6:50 report it the same week: *"the new update made the rock a phantom. Can't land on it anymore."* ([comment on the 6:50](https://youtu.be/6WEIHo7AUf0?t=0))

Both closures sit in **the Sanctum**, the split where ***TexasPete*** spent **119.38 s** and ***Nanobit*** **116.15 s**. That is exactly where the new tech lands.

## What replaced them?

**Flight.** ***Dotxy*** posted a Sanctum route **the same day** the patch shipped:

> "Heyo, here's a new way to get through Kuldra's Sanctum quick in the Runescape Dragonwilds 1.0.1.2 hotfix!" ([Sanctum flight](https://youtu.be/GeWbo4GpFEg?t=0))

The loop, in ***Dotxy's*** own steps:

1. Windstep
2. Jump Attack
3. `Q` (Spell Ring)
4. Any targeted AoE spell
5. `Esc` (closes the Spell Ring)
6. Jump

"Repeat steps 2-6 as needed!" Learn it in **[What is the infinite double jump?](/infinite-double-jump-sanctum-flight/)** and route it in **[How do you fly through Kuldra's Sanctum?](/kuldras-sanctum-flight-and-fight-skip/)**.

## Is there a faster lead?

***IINo1goodII*** posted **"Kuldra skipped in 4 mins 40 seconds"** on 28 October, after 1.0.1.2:

> "Sub 4 mins is possible. I'm confident someone could do it in around 3 minutes flat. Not showing full clip or methods used.. sorry guys." ([video](https://youtu.be/4hncfjhUvg8?t=0))

In a comment he outlines the method: *"I launched myself skipping the 3rd checkpoint and some of the parkour area. Used the plunge mech to bypass the rocks on the fight itself since they are patched. Surge dashed the bridge."*

The run sits **off speedrun.com** and its timing method is unstated, so treat it as **a direction to explore**: a launch past the third checkpoint, plunge past the rocks, Surge across the bridge. Ask in the **[RSDW Speedrunning Discord](https://discord.gg/aGZkuC5yK6)**, which ***Nanobit*** names as the hub.

## What should you re-test today?

1. **The Stronghold launch.** Copy ***FastTipsGaming's*** climb left of the air door three times. It is the newest save on the route and ties ***TexasPete's*** patched climb (51.7 s). Keep ***Sir_Rebral's*** Thane kill as the fallback.
2. **Back-to-back attempts.** On repeat runs the Commander teleport can drop you through the floor. ***Bazilleos***: *"Happens every time you repeat final boss. Commander teleports you to the next platform and in doing so you spawn in middle of the ground and fall down to the death"* ([video](https://youtu.be/JuuIF0BeGaE?t=0)). Plan resets in **[How do you reset for back-to-back raid attempts?](/reset-discipline-back-to-back-attempts/)**.
3. **Your patch number.** Confirm the title screen reads 1.0.1.2 before every session. **1.0.1.3** is referenced by ***Nanobit*** and by NRS NEO ([video](https://youtu.be/0HeTn4G7oVk?t=0)) with no Steam post, so it is likely a silent hotfix.


## What this means when you run

- Route the **Sanctum with flight**; the block climb and the rock are closed on 1.0.1.2.
- Keep the **Kuldra fight skip** in the route; it is confirmed twice after the patch.
- Route the **Keep hole**; ***FastTipsGaming's*** 2 Nov run shows it working after the patch.
