**Raid practice** is the loop that turns a skip you **landed once** into a skip you **land every attempt**: **drill one skip in isolation**, **log each make and miss**, **reset cleanly**, then **link the skips** into full runs against the record.

**Consistency decides this record.** ***Nanobit*** says of the Vines-to-Fragments zoop: *"At least half of my runs died here."* ([comment on the 7:01](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=105)).

## Why does consistency decide the record?

**The record route is a chain of single-attempt skips.** ***TexasPete*** described it after his 7:40: *"If you have tried this, you know there is so many places that one small mistake will blow the run."* ([7:40, description](https://youtu.be/6N3NwYA5Zh0?t=0)).

**Clean execution is the gap to sub 7.** ***Nanobit*** on his 7:04: *"Overall just doing things more cleanly will produce a sub 7 considering my sum of best is 6:58."* ([7:04, description](https://youtu.be/KljLK0Z-9jY?t=0)).

## What are the parts of a practice system?

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Drill** | Repeating one skip on its own until it lands on demand. |
| **Make rate** | Makes divided by attempts for one skip. |
| **Weakest skip** | The skip with the lowest make rate; you drill it next. |
| **Gold** | Your best ever time for one split. |
| **Sum of best** | All your golds added together: your ceiling today. |
| **Reset** | Returning the raid and your character to a clean start state. |

## What does a practice session look like?

1. **Set up** from the [pre-run checklist](/raid-speedrun-setup-checklist/), with a backup of your character file.
2. **Warm up the movement tech** on flat ground: dive cancel, then the flight loop.
3. **Drill your weakest skip** for a fixed block, logging every make and miss.
4. **Drill the Keep hole or the Sanctum flight**, whichever is weaker.
5. **Run full attempts**, split every one against **1:22.70 / 2:23.20 / 3:15.70 / 4:42.30 / 6:48.10**, and reset on the [reset thresholds](/reset-discipline-back-to-back-attempts/).
6. **Review** where runs die and pick tomorrow's weakest skip.

## Which build do you practise on?

**Learn on the safe learning route, then graduate to the record route.** The Raid Lab's **safe learning build** follows ***SamFPlays'*** 1.0.1 follow-along and is written *"for new runners learning the skips one at a time; consistent before fast"*. The **record build** pairs ***Nanobit's*** 7:01 route with ***Dotxy's*** Sanctum flight. Both list the same unlocks: Agility 44, Magic 11, Attack 25 and Magical Mending at Artisan 56 ([requirements](/what-do-you-need-to-speedrun-the-scorned-wilderness-raid/)).

## Where does the time spread come from?

**The Keep and the Sanctum hold most of the variation.** Across the six frame-measured reference runs (TexasPete 6:50 and 7:40, Nanobit 7:01, Nimblesk, ChiefWes, SamFPlays), the Raid Lab measured each split's share of the total variance:

| Split | Share of total variance (measured) |
|---|---|
| **Dragontail Vines** | 15.3% |
| **Fragments of Ghornfell** | 13.7% |
| **The Shattered Stronghold** | 15.2% |
| **Cataclysm Keep** | 29.5% |
| **Kuldra's Sanctum** | 26.4% |

**Spend your drill time where the spread is**: the Keep hole and the Sanctum flight.

## How do you get more attempts per hour on PC?

**Force-close after a failed zoop.** A reply under ***Nanobit's*** 7:01: *"If you force close your game process after failing the zoop, you can reset raid progress to not-started to get more attempts without having to complete the entire raid every time. ... I recommend backing up your character json if you do this, just in-case."* ([reply on the 7:01](https://youtu.be/doVQ9Ph256g?t=105)). **Back up your character file first**, then use it for Vines practice.

## What this means when you run

- **Log every attempt** on every skip so your weakest skip is a number.
- **Drill before you run**, then run full attempts with the skips warm.
- **Compare your sum of best** to 6:50 to see what clean execution is worth.
